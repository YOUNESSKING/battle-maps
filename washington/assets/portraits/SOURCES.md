CAVEATS:
- **Mercer:** the Met titles this sheet "Hugh Mercer, Jr.": it is the general's SON (Trumbull used the young Hugh Mercer Jr. as the model for the dying general). It is NOT a likeness of Brig. Gen. Hugh Mercer (c. 1726-1777). No authenticated life portrait of the general was found in the public domain; the Wikipedia/Commons images tagged "Hugh Mercer" are other people (Peregrine Hopson; Confederate Hugh W. Mercer). Options: show Trumbull's painting "The Death of General Mercer at the Battle of Princeton" (Yale; Commons File:The Death of General Mercer at the Battle of Princeton January 3 1777.jpeg, listed in the Hugh Mercer Wikipedia article) or a sepia-treated Saint-Memin profile drawing of Mercer in the National Gallery of Art (https://www.nga.gov/artworks/215127-hugh-tenant-weedon-mercer is the SON; check before use). Do not present the Met drawing as the general.
- **Rall:** the only widely used image is the engraved detail "Col. Rahl from Trumbull's Battle of Trenton" (NYPL b12349149-em5598), Wikipedia's infobox image; it is from John Trumbull's 1790s print, an imagined likeness made after his death, NOT from life (no portrait of Rall from life is known). Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Col._Rahl_from_Trumbull%27s_Battle_of_Trenton_(NYPL_b12349149-em5598).jpg ; original https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d5/Col._Rahl_from_Trumbull%27s_Battle_of_Trenton_%28NYPL_b12349149-em5598%29.jpg (2761x3630, PD).
