# Distant bomb impact SFX candidates — Goose Green Harrier strike

All 6 candidates below are genuine recorded sound (no synthesized/fireworks material). Both
sources are part of the same lineage: professional optical/magnetic sound-effects elements shot
for Hollywood features from the 1930s-60s, held by the USC Cinema / Sunset Editorial Collection
and digitized/released as CC0. craigsmith uploaded a subset to Freesound; the full "REAL" folder
(genuine recorded elements, as opposed to the library's separate "DESIGNED"/"MISC" folders) is
also mirrored on Internet Archive with an explicit CC0 licence URL on the item page. Nothing here
duplicates the previously-found Freesound IDs 345844, 108640, 171475, 320788, 455512.

Do NOT use the previous search's finds; do NOT touch files outside this folder.

## Trim spec applied

Every `*_hit.wav` / `*_stick.wav` is 48 kHz / 16-bit PCM, peak-normalized to exactly **-3.0 dBFS**,
starts with no leading silence, and ends with a short 0.3 s fade-out over its natural tail.

single hit, a 4 s gap, the same hit again, then (if present) a 1 s gap and the stick excerpt.
Beep start times are listed above per candidate.
