## Best vivid details that ARE verified
1. Grant to Halleck, 11 May, 8:30 AM: "...fight it out on this line if it takes all summer." (Memoirs, dispatch printed in full.)
2. Grant's own judgment: "I have always regretted that the last assault at Cold Harbor was ever made ... no advantage whatever was gained to compensate for the heavy loss we sustained." (Memoirs.)
3. Rhea: the 3 June charge "lasted less than an hour" and cost "about 3,500" Union casualties; about 12,000 Union casualties in the whole Cold Harbor fortnight (NPS).
4. The move began "after dark on the evening of the 12th"; 115,000 men by Grant's own count; Hancock first, by ferry, morning of 14 June.
5. The bridge: 101 pontoons, about 2,100 feet, tidal river with 70-90 feet of water in the channel, anchored to vessels, started 4 PM, ready about 11 PM on 14 June; "perhaps the longest pontoon bridge in military history" (McPherson). 
6. Robbins's engineer count of the cost of speed: army crossed "without the loss of a single man or animal"; most in 47 hours.
7. Lee's 14 June telegram: "Should enemys whole army cross to south side this army can cross also. We must be wary & not be deceived by enemys movements." Then 16 June: "has Grant been seen crossing James River"; 17 June 4:30 PM: "Have no information of Grant's crossing James River."
8. 15 June: about 14,000 men of the XVIII Corps (Hinks's colored troops in the lead) against Beauregard's 2,200; "opened up a two mile-long hole in the Dimmock Line"; Smith waited for the 16th (NPS). Grant: "I believed then, and still believe, that Petersburg could have been easily captured at that time."
9. Result: 15-18 June cost the Union about 11,400 men for no capture; Lee reached Petersburg about noon on 18 June; siege "over nine months".

## Do not say
- Do not say "55,000 in thirty days"; say about 55,000 in the Overland Campaign, or "in six weeks".
- Do not say June 3 lasted "several hours" or cost "4,000"; say "under an hour, about 3,500 by Rhea's count".
- Do not state the pinned-name-slips story as fact (single source Porter, 30 years later; Rhea calls it suspect).
- Do not use "I regret this assault more than any one I have ever ordered" as Grant's Memoirs text.
- Do not say "the bridge at Wilcox's Landing": ferries at Wilcox's Landing, bridge at Weyanoke/Fort Powhatan.
- Do not say "450 engineers", "85 feet", "3 schooners", "8 hours", "50-mile wagon train", "5 railroads" as documented facts.
- Do not say "fifty yards from Lee's eyes", "widest river in Virginia", "secretly" (Lee guessed by 14 June).
- Do not quote Lee as "I have not heard of Grant's crossing the James River" on 16 June 3 PM; use the 17 June 4:30 PM text, or the 16 June question.
- Do not say Smith had 16,000 or captured "16 guns" or "1.5 miles"; say about 14,000, "several guns", "about two miles".
- Do not say General Orders No. 11 was issued at Holly Springs (Oxford, Miss.), and do not use 15.4% / 20.2% as a matched Grant/Lee pair.
