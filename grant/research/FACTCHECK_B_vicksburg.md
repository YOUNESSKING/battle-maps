## Best vivid details that ARE verified
1. 16 Apr 1863, 10 p.m.: seven gunboats and three transports slide past Vicksburg; Confederates fire houses and light bonfires to make the river bright; the Henry Clay burns to the water's edge and drifts down the river; Grant watched "from the deck of a river transport." Nobody on the transports was killed. (GM)
2. Sherman hauled four yawl-boats across the swamp and manned them with soldiers to rescue anyone from the wrecks. (SM)
3. Army volunteers manned the 22 Apr supply steamers: "enough presented themselves to take five times the number." (GM)
4. 29 Apr: Porter's eight gunboats pound Grand Gulf for five and a half hours and silence not one gun while 10,000 soldiers wait on transports. (GM)
5. That night a Black man tells Grant of a good landing at Bruinsburg and a road to Port Gibson; the landing next morning is unopposed. Grant: "I felt a degree of relief scarcely ever equalled since." (GM)
6. Grant's dispatch to Sherman about rations: "make the country furnish the balance"; the army left Bruinsburg with about two days' rations; plantation corn mills ran day and night. (GM)
7. "I therefore determined to move independently of Banks, cut loose from my base." (GM) Grant also notes Sherman, who "afterwards ignored bases of supplies," had warned him.
8. Five battles, Port Gibson to Big Black, for 695 killed, 3,425 wounded, 259 missing; Grant says he beat 60,000 "in detail by a force smaller than their own."
9. Champion Hill: Hovey's division loses over a third of its men; Pemberton loses Loring's whole division.
10. 4 Jul 1863: 10 a.m. white flags, men in gray stack arms; Grant gave parole rather than demand unconditional surrender; 172 cannon and about 60,000 muskets taken. Lincoln: "The Father of Waters again goes unvexed to the sea." (NPS)
11. McClure/Lincoln: Lincoln sat silent a long time, then said "I can't spare this man; he fights." Lincoln had never met Grant. (MC)
12. Grant on the 22 May assault: "I have always regretted that the last assault at Cold Harbor was ever made. I might say the same thing of the assault of the 22d of May, 1863, at Vicksburg." (GM)

## Do not say
- Do not say Sherman's 8 April letter was a protest against cutting loose or against crossing at Bruinsburg. It is a real letter proposing another plan, and Sherman himself rejects the word "protest". Use his Hankinson's Ferry advice and Grant's "make the country furnish the balance" reply.
- Do not give 24,000 landed, or 41,000-44,000 for Grant. Use about 17,000 on the first day, about 33,000 by 7 May (Grant).
- Do not say 29,491; say about 29,500 (29,495 per NPS-based figures; Grant wrote 31,600).
- Do not give "180-200 miles", or say Grant carried no baggage or only a toothbrush, as fact.
- Do not say "only general in history" to force three surrenders; say "three surrenders: Donelson, Vicksburg, Appomattox".
- Do not give the Shiloh "Lick 'em tomorrow" exchange as recorded by Grant, Sherman or Reid in 1879. It is unsourced folklore in the sources we could read.
- Do not say Lincoln's whiskey barrel line is documented; it first appears in newspapers in September 1863 and Lincoln reportedly denied it.
- Do not say the Yazoo supply line "opened 18 May" as one date; roads were built between the 19 and 22 May assaults.
- Do not name the Black guide or invent his words; do not name Streight in the Vicksburg diversions.
- Do not state a casualty figure for the 19 and 22 May assaults or a precise date for the 6-7 June episode without a source.
- Do not say "five gunboats" or "eight gunboats" for the 16 Apr run: seven gunboats plus three transports (eight gunboats were at the 29 Apr Grand Gulf attack).
