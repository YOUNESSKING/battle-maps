Peng Dehuai's Top 3 Legendary Tactical Moves | The Man Who Drove America Out of North Korea

Three hundred thousand Chinese soldiers marched into North Korea in secret, and not one unit was spotted from the air. Within a month, the US Eighth Army had been driven out of North Korea, in what the US Army itself calls the longest retreat in its history.

Peng Dehuai commanded the Chinese People's Volunteers in Korea. In this video we break down his three greatest tactical moves: the night ambush of the US 8th Cavalry Regiment at Unsan in November 1950, the trap on the Chongchon River and the Kunu-ri "Gauntlet" that shattered the Eighth Army's right flank in late November 1950, and the New Year's Eve offensive against the seams of the UN line that took Seoul in January 1951.

Own the night. Bait the trap. Hit the seams.
