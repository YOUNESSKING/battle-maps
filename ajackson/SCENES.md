# Andrew Jackson film: scene direction (read with script.md, the shot tags are the shot list)

Scene windows (`python3 ajackson/tools/scene_times.py SNN --words`): S01 83.69 s · S02 46.51 · S03 177.69 · S04 71.11 · S05 148.07 · S06 103.93 · S07 152.86 · S08 65.61.
Worlds (`ajackson/worlds/README.md`): gulf (z8 wide), neworleans (z10 wide), chalmette (z10 close), horseshoe (z11 close). Example `ajackson/sequences/_smoke/seq.js`.

## Global rules (owner, binding)
- **Only 3 cloud dives in the whole film:** S01 (gulf -> chalmette), S03 (gulf -> horseshoe), S05 (neworleans -> chalmette at night). Every other region change = a 0.9 s dissolve (`dissolve` on a cut key) or a hard cut where the script tag says "hard cut".
- **Troops = line blocks** exactly as `vicksburg/sequences/S10_gm` (`troops: "blocks"`, `troopsOpts: { look: "lineblocks" }`): every unit is a WIDE rectangle that moves only long side first (never lengthwise, no columns); stop -> turn in place -> walk straight; retreats = about-face then walk back; no stretching, no collisions, no pops. Narrow crossings (canoes, the Bienvenue boats): men break rank gradually, stream across and continue. Run `node vicksburg/tools/check_troops_motion.mjs ajackson/scenes/SNN 0 D <region>` on every scene and fix every flag (turn rate, overlaps, pops, shape).
- **Muzzle flashes:** every musket volley / cannon shot shows a small visible flash at the exact shot time (engine support added by the S05 author; use `smokeLines`/`volleys`/`guns` normally). Sound belongs only to something visible: no fx off screen.
- **Muzzle flashes ready:** nothing to do: every `fx.volleys` / `fx.smokeLines` entry (give it `volley` + `roll` for volleys) and every `fx.guns` shot now shows visible flashes at its exact shot time (night: + a warm ground light). The volley's first flash = its export time (`t0 + v * volley`, starting at `pts[0]`), the ripple runs to the last point over `roll`; `export_troop_events.mjs` lists every flash (`shots`) and `troop_sfx.py` puts one shot sound on each. Tune with `opts.muzzleFlash: { size, every, life }`, switch off with `opts.flash: false` or `SEQ.flashes: false`. Only fire where the camera looks (no flash = no sound).
- **Sides:** blue `u` = Americans + their Cherokee / Lower Creek allies; red `r` = British and (S02-S03) the Red Stick Creeks. Banners on poles, one per side on screen: Jackson (Jarvis portrait, US 15-star flag `ajackson/assets/flags/us15_flag.png`), Coffee (move 1, S05), Pakenham (S05-S07, Union Flag 1801 `ajackson/assets/flags/gb1801_union_flag.png`), Red Sticks: a banner with NO portrait (initials RS or a plain cloth) and a plain dark-red pennant, Menawa portrait only if his name is spoken (it is not). Keane has no PD portrait: initials. Portraits in `ajackson/assets/portraits/` (cut to 400x400 RGBA like `vicksburg/engine/assets/generals/`, rembg isnet + clean matte). Banner side palette: pass `side`/flag so British banners get the red palette.
- **Nothing on screen before its spoken word** (labels, cards, stamps, banners, numbers, dates). One big card at a time; labels leave after ~3.5 s. Date plaque rolls on each spoken date (year only until a day is spoken).
- Cards (number, result, method, quote, treaty) use the engine overlay devices (`number`, `medallion`, `ledger`, `letter`, `quote`, `title`). Method card "THE OLD HICKORY METHOD" grows one line per move: S03 line 1, S05 line 2, S07 line 3 (same design each time).
- Engagement (STYLE_LOCK 1b) built once as overlay devices `O.engage.subscribe / decide / final` (by the S03 author, in `vicksburg/engine/overlay.js`, backward compatible, documented): S03 subscribe button on "subscribing"; S05 WHAT WOULD YOU DO? (A: DIG IN AND WAIT / B: ATTACK TONIGHT, reveal B on "He did not wait"); S08 final 1 / 2 / 3 cards + subscribe.
- **Engagement devices ready** (S03 author, `vicksburg/engine/README.md` last section): `["engage.subscribe", t, until]` (click at t + 1.25), `["engage.decide", { sub, a, b, real: "b", t, tCount, tReveal, until }]` (dims the map itself), `["engage.final", { moves: [["HORSESHOE BEND", "27 MARCH 1814"], ["THE NIGHT ATTACK", "23 DECEMBER 1814"], ["THE MUD LINE", "8 JANUARY 1815"]], tShow, tNums, tAsk, tSub, until, extra, tExtra }]`. Also new: boats `kind: "canoe" | "barge" | "ship"`; `dives[].overTo` (over point in the target region's units: needed for gulf z8 -> close dives, else the emergence looks at the wrong place); `O.letter(..., { qAt })`.
- **S02 -> S03 hand-over (S03 author, for the S01/S02 author):** S03's first frame = gulf camera `at: [OV[0] - 10, OV[1] + 26], dist: 300, tilt: 36, yaw: 0, fov: 30` with `OV = VX.place("Horseshoe Bend (Tohopeka", "gulf")` (~[1087.8, 160.4]); S02's last key (its final 0.6 s dissolves into S03) should sit on exactly this camera, with no units/labels/banners left on screen at the end. S03 ends on gulf `at: [640, 760], dist: 860, tilt: 38` (= S04's first key).
- **Method card recipe (S03 line 1)**: `["letter", tIn, tOut, { header: "Move 1 · Horseshoe Bend", title: "The Old Hickory Method", sub: "", marks: false, quote: "1. Close every door", qSize: 34, cps: 14, qAt: <word>, lag: 0.05, stagger: 0.06, partDur: 0.35 }]`; S05/S07: same, earlier lines first in the quote with `\n` (e.g. `quote: "1. Close every door\n2. ..."`, `height: 260`) and qAt so the new line types on its word (or put the old lines in `sub`).
- Respect: Horseshoe Bend is not a slaughter spectacle. Show the assault and the river closed, then the result card over a still, dimmed map; no lingering fire on fleeing people. No gore, no celebration stamps.
- No modern features, no reservoirs; weather only where history says: fog on the morning of 8 Jan (lifting as the British close), night + fog/smoke on 23 Dec, foggy morning 1 Jan; otherwise clear winter light (spring at Horseshoe Bend).
- Readability at 1280x720: blocks ~2-4 % of frame width; never lose blocks in the cypress woods; labels not under clouds.

## Per scene (beyond the shot tags)
- S01: frame 0 = the gulf from altitude with the British fleet (boats) already moving; the dive lands on the plain on "eighth of January"; the 8 Jan clash must read in ~10 s (lines, flashes, the break); GHENT treaty = an overlay card (parchment/letter device) over a dimmed map; the 23 Dec flash-back on the chalmette region (day); 3D gold title ANDREW JACKSON on "His name was Andrew Jackson" (Cinzel font); 3 snap cuts: horseshoe region, chalmette night, chalmette line with smoke. Regions: gulf, chalmette, horseshoe.
- S02: gulf only, slow drift north-east from the Gulf coast to the Creek country; Fort Mims pulse; ends looking at the Tallapoosa (S03's dive starts on the gulf).
- S03: dive 2 at the start onto the bend; the wall across the neck, Gun Hill guns (2), Coffee's mounted blocks + Cherokee / Lower Creek blocks across the river all round the bend, canoes (boats) taken across at the toe, the village burning (house fires), the charge over the wall, result card, treaty card on the gulf map (Creek cession shaded), method line 1, Jackson banner moves to Mobile, subscribe. Regions: gulf, horseshoe.
- S04: gulf -> neworleans (dissolve): fleet from the south-east, Pensacola, Jackson to New Orleans (1 Dec), Lake Borgne gunboats (5 blue boats captured grey on 14 Dec), the British boats up Bayou Bienvenue, advance guard out of the swamp onto Villeré's (chalmette, day) late morning 23 Dec; Villeré's son escapes (a small marker running to the river, a pirogue across).
- S05: chalmette by day, quote card, decision card (dimmed), dive 3 neworleans -> chalmette NIGHT (night 1); campfires on the British camp; the Carolina (boat on the river) drifts down and anchors opposite the camp; broadside flashes; Jackson's columns down the river road, Coffee's swing out by the swamp edge; night melee in small groups with flashes and smoke; dawn grey withdrawal to the Rodriguez line; result card; Pakenham banner on his name; method line 2.
- S06: the line rising (works growing or blocks digging), 8 batteries placed one by one, the defenders filing in, the Carolina burning (27 Dec), the 28 Dec probe beaten back, the 1 Jan artillery duel in fog (sugar-barrel batteries), Pakenham's three-part plan as arrows (west bank, river column, main column), LADDERS stamp.
- S07: 8 Jan fog + rocket; boats late on the west bank; the main column toward the swamp end, the river column at the advanced redoubt, the 93rd crossing the field and standing; batteries then rank fire; NO LADDERS; Pakenham's banner falls; HALF AN HOUR; west bank break-through and recall; result card; method line 3.
- S08: gulf: the fleet leaves; treaty APPROVED; Jackson banner; the Creek lands + muted removal arrows west across the Mississippi; scar card callback; final cards + subscribe; the map stays alive for the end screen (last ~20 s clean).
