# Critic review: Jackson thumbnails (A MAIN "Back Door", B "Grand Maneuver")

## A MAIN: findings
1. **Arrow logic is wrong/ambiguous.** The white sweep starts under the red British columns and ends on the blue line, so it reads as the British attack, not Jackson's move. The hero's move is "he dug", and the dug thing is not visible at all: the rampart is a thin dark hairline, no canal, no earthwork. The viewer cannot tell what was dug. Fix: draw a thick dark-brown earth rampart bar (full width of the blue line, ~14 px high, with shadow) plus a 3 px blue-grey canal strip in front of it; tag it "THE MUD WALL" (replace or add to the parchment tags). Make the arrow start at/near New Orleans or the rampart and loop to the wall, or remove the arrow's overlap with the red blocks.
2. **Edge fringe on the portrait.** Warm tan/orange outline around the hair and cheek (x 950-1130, y 170-330) and along the right shoulder. Looks like a matte halo. Shrink the matte by 2-3 px, kill the rim colour on the face, keep the rim only on the shoulder side.
3. **Portrait is a flat, low-contrast engraving sepia.** Sepia on dark is per recipe, but the face is grey-pale and mushy at 168 px. Raise contrast (curves, +20 % on the mid-tones), add a subtle left-edge shadow so it sits in the scene. The hard black panel from x ~950 on kills the landscape (the swamp flank is gone), so the "river on one side, swamp on the other" geometry is lost. Start the dark fade later (x ~1010) and keep a hint of cypress on the right.
4. **Tags collide with blocks.** "BRITISH ARMY" covers the first red column; "NEW ORLEANS" floats over empty field. Move BRITISH ARMY to x 40-330, y 520-ish above the band, or onto the left of the columns; put NEW ORLEANS beside the blue line. Tags are fine (torn parchment) but are unreadable at 168 px (the strip shows blobs): increase serif size ~20 %.
5. **Blocks.** Red columns look correct for history (columns across open fields), with shadows. The blue line has no visible shadow, and the blocks are tiny and uniform. Add the same drop shadow and make the line thicker (2 rows). Ships on the river are sailing ships in the sun glow; fine, but the sun and glare are brighter than the rest of the image and pull the eye to the top left.
6. Band and word: matches the reference well. The word is readable at 168 px. Keep.

## B: findings
1. **Portrait repaint looks AI-made:** greenish-grey hair, patchy skin, blue-yellow glow halo around the head and a cyan fringe along the shoulder edges. Reduce the outer glow to a 2 px warm rim, recolour the hair to a consistent grey-white, add a drop shadow instead of glow. Compare Marion B: face cleaner, helmet fully lit.
2. **Bottom-right label "THE MUD WALL" hugs the corner (about 30 px margin) and is tiny at 168 px.** Move left/up ~40 px, enlarge ~25 %. "NEW ORLEANS" is fine.
3. **The arrow is a stubby chubby curve with a weak head.** The Marion arrow is longer and glows. Lengthen it (start at the seal edge x 770, end at x 905), thin the shaft, and add a light blue glow.
4. **Payoff icon: wall has a picket fence and cotton bales.** The 1815 line was a plain earth rampart behind a ditch; cotton bales were pulled out. Simplify: remove the fence, make the rampart longer and straighter, keep the cannon and puffs. Add a visible canal in front (it does have a reflection, good).
5. Empty band between the portrait and the icon (x 400-620, y 330-700). Fine, but could host nothing; do not add clutter. Subline "THEN THE BRITISH ATTACKED" is a good twist; at 168 px it is borderline, raise size ~10 %.
6. Good: word size and cream colour match Marion B. Hairline rivers are faint but present.

## History checks
- British attacked in columns toward a rampart behind a canal: A shows columns and a line, but no rampart or canal visible. Add them.
- Mississippi on one flank and swamp on the other: A shows only the river; the swamp is blacked out.
- No flags shown, so the 15-star rule is not triggered. If a flag is added, 15 stars and 15 stripes.

## Top fixes in order of impact
1. A: add a visible earthwork + canal and "THE MUD WALL" tag; fix arrow ownership.
2. A and B: clean portrait edges (halo/fringe).
3. B: repaint-quality fixes on the face and hair, shadow not glow.
4. A: tag collisions and bigger tag text for feed size.
5. B: label margin and size, longer glowing arrow, remove the fence and cotton bales.
