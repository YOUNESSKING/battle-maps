J. Lawton Collins's Top 3 Legendary Tactical Moves | The Man Who Broke Open Normandy

Fifteen hundred heavy bombers. More than three thousand tons of bombs on a strip of Normandy barely three and a half miles wide, and some of them fell on American soldiers. Within a week the German front in Normandy had been torn open, and the man who broke it was not Patton.

J. Lawton "Lightning Joe" Collins commanded the US VII Corps from Utah Beach to the Elbe. In this video we break down his three greatest tactical moves: cutting off and storming the fortress port of Cherbourg in June 1944, throwing his armour into the chaos of Operation Cobra to break out of Normandy in July 1944, and destroying the spearhead of the 2nd Panzer Division at Celles, just short of the Meuse, on Christmas Day 1944.

Cut them off, then never let them dig in. Throw the tanks into the chaos. Hit the spearhead when it runs dry.
