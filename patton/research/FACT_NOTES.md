## Corrections to Gemini (biggest first)
1. **"48 hours"** is wrong: Patton's diary, "On December 22 with three divisions" (about 3 days). No evidence of a rejected Dec 21 offer. Ike's "Don't be fatuous, George" is a secondary anecdote: script says "reportedly".
2. **"Play ball"**: real, but for 7 Nov 1944 (Cole), not Dec 19, and Bradley did not authorize it. For Dec 19 the sources say only "a simple code"; Cent/Nickel/Dime come only from Hymel. Not used in the script; banner PLAY BALL is NOT usable.
3. **"Let the bastards go all the way to Paris"**: not in Patton's Dec 19 diary, unsourced popular line. NOT used.
4. **Dec 18 plans**: Patton did not pre-order three plans on Dec 18. Koch warned Dec 9; Bradley summoned him Dec 18; three plans drawn the night of 18-19 / morning of 19 Dec. Patton first misjudged the attack (his own Dec 17 diary words).
5. **German strength**: Cole = over 200,000 men, about 500 medium tanks (not 250,000/1,000). "Separate peace" not in Cole.
6. **Verdun**: a freezing French army barracks at 11:00, not a bunker. Attack date Dec 22, three divisions (4th Armd, 26th, 80th).
7. **Avranches**: Pontaubault (not Pontlaubert), not the only crossing; "7 divisions / 100,000 men / 20,000 vehicles in 72 hours" NOT in the official history. Patton directing traffic and the Michelin maps are unsourced. "Another Dunkirk" quote unsourced.
8. **Argentan**: official history calls it Bradley's "shorter envelopment" / right hook, not a "90-degree left wheel". Bradley (not Patton) halted XV Corps on 13 Aug; Patton called it "a great mistake". Cobra: Bradley's plan, Collins's VII Corps; VIII Corps (Middleton) took Avranches.
9. **Numbers dropped (no source)**: Third Army Aug casualties 15,724 / 68,500 POWs; 133,178 vehicles / 4.5M vehicle-miles / 419,581 tons; 101st casualties 341/1,691/516 (Wikipedia only); "11,000 inside Bastogne" (Cole gives no total); 1,900 prisoners and 5x7-mile bridgehead at Oppenheim.
10. **Bastogne link-up**: 13 batteries, 10 volleys (not four batteries rolling); Teller mines hit behind the first tanks; at 16:50 Boggess met the 326th Engineers; Abrams (not Boggess) shook McAuliffe's hand 20 minutes later. "400 yards", megaphone line, "MacAuliffe Rescued by Patton" caption: unsourced. Patton reached Bastogne 30 Dec. Weather-prayer cards issued 22 Dec; clear weather 23 Dec; "That O'Neill sure did some potent praying" (WAKI footnote).
11. **Rhine**: first boats crossed at NIERSTEIN about 22:30 (half an hour late), K Co, 3rd Bn, 11th Inf, 1st Lt Irven Jacobs; A and B Cos crossed at Oppenheim under MG fire. Lemmon, Company I, Sgt Chilson, 748th Tank Bn DD tanks are unsupported. Casualties: 11th Inf 20 (MacDonald), Patton's diary tally 28; 8 killed/20 wounded split unverified. Planes: Patton claimed 33, official 19. Mainz feint used smoke; Weyland flew cover (so "zero smoke/air" is wrong). Hausser commanded Army Group G until 2 Apr. Mar 24 bridge: with Eddy, Codman, Stiller (not Gay); diary words "to take a piss ... in the Rhine" and "in emulation of William the Conqueror"; "See! I have taken the land in both my hands" is invented.
12. **Controversies**: Sicily 3 and 10 Aug 1943 (Kuhl, Bennett); say "combat exhaustion", Eisenhower "reprimanded"; Task Force Baum = 293 men plus Stiller, 25-27 Mar (not 314/57); Driant: Walker pushed it. In the script only the Sicily slaps appear, once, factually.
