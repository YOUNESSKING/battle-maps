## OLD background (38 s, 99 words) -> NEW background (about 20 s, 55 words, only what move 1 needs)
OLD: George Patton was born in California in eighteen eighty-five, and he spent his life in the cavalry and in tanks. In nineteen forty-three, in Sicily, he slapped two hospitalised soldiers, and Eisenhower reprimanded him for it. When the Allies landed in Normandy the next summer, he had no army to command. Then, at noon on the first of August, nineteen forty-four, his Third Army came to life, at the end of a breakout that other men had made possible. Patton marked the birthday of his new army with a bottle of brandy that he and an aide found undrinkable.
NEW: For weeks after D-Day, the Allies had been bottled up in the hedgerows of Normandy. Patton had no army in the field until the first of August, nineteen forty-four, when his Third Army came to life at noon. To get out, it first had to squeeze through a corner of Normandy only a few miles wide.
