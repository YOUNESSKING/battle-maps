# New SFX credits (O.P. Smith video)

Source: freesound.org (licence read on each sound's page). Only CC0 and CC BY used.
Processing: mono, 48 kHz, trimmed, 40 ms fades (loops: none, crossfaded seam), peak-normalised to -3 dBFS
(same as the goosegreen library). Distance treatment (low-pass, light echo) on naval_gun, bugle, truck, bulldozer, ship_1.
Files are committed as .flac (lossless; WAVs would be 30 MB). Convert to 48 kHz WAV before use:
`ffmpeg -i x.flac x.wav` (the WAVs are git-ignored). Suggested levels (dB): naval_gun -9, bugle -18, blizzard -28 (bed),
truck -20, parachute -14, bulldozer -22, ship -20, impact -7, explosion -4.
