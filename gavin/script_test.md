[MAP: bz-2 | Gavin badge, scattered drop, takes the ridge 08:30] Sicily, the eleventh of July, 1943. The night drop had scattered Colonel James Gavin's paratroopers all over the island. [MAP: bz-3 | German counterattack: 700 infantry + Tigers; bazookas, pack howitzers, front line] That afternoon, the Hermann Goering Division struck back. Bazooka rounds barely dented the Tigers. [MAP: bz-4 | navy gunfire from offshore, Shermans arrive, counterattack, Germans pull back] Then a young navy ensign with a radio called in the warships offshore.
