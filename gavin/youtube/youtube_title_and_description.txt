Paratroopers vs Tiger Tanks: James Gavin's Top 3 Legendary Tactical Moves

Twenty-six canvas boats. Two hundred and sixty men. A river three hundred metres wide, in broad daylight, with German guns waiting on the far bank. The general who sent them had done this kind of thing before.

In this video we break down James Gavin's three greatest tactical moves: the stand on Biazza Ridge against Tiger tanks in Sicily (1943), the charge across the La Fière causeway in Normandy (1944), and the Waal River crossing at Nijmegen during Operation Market Garden (1944).

Lead from the front. Take the ground that decides the battle. Attack where the enemy is sure you cannot.
