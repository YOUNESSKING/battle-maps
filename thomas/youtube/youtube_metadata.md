## Description
September 20, 1863: because of one confusing order, half of a Union army broke and ran in the woods of north Georgia, and its commander rode away convinced everything was lost. One quiet Virginian stayed.

In this video we break down George H. Thomas's three greatest tactical moves: the stand on Horseshoe Ridge that earned him the name "the Rock of Chickamauga", the charge up Missionary Ridge that nobody ordered, and the battle of Nashville, where Grant was about to relieve him of command days before he crushed John Bell Hood's army.

Refuse to be hurried. Hold the ground that matters. Strike to destroy, not just to defeat.
