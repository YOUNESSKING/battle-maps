## Description

On September 20, 1863, half of a Union army broke and ran in the woods of north Georgia, and its commander rode away convinced everything was lost. One quiet Virginian stayed. This is the story of George H. Thomas, the Rock of Chickamauga: the stand on Horseshoe Ridge, the charge up Missionary Ridge that nobody ordered, and the hammer blow at Nashville that destroyed a Confederate army, days before Grant could fire him.
