## Hard-defect checks (all 8)
- Flags: none anywhere, no saltire. Pass.
- Banner spelling: exact on all 8. OUTNUMBERED without quotes on 01-04, 08. Pass.
- Small labels: clean on 02-08; garbled on 01.
- Pin faces: wrong Cleburne on 01 and 05. 02, 06, 07, 08 pins are a bearded general consistent with the main face.
- Arrows: correct (red -> blue, stopping at the line) on 02, 05, 06, 07, 08. Wrong on 03; muddled on 04.
- **Pin-frame stars (02, 05-08):** a ~8 px pale/gold 5-point star in the top-left corner of each pin frame.
  Invisible at 320 and 168 px; at full size it reads as frame decoration, not an emblem. On Sherman's red frame
  (02, 07, 08) red field + star is a faint Soviet echo only if someone zooms in. Not a blocker; worth one cheap refine on the pick (below).
- Colour code note (not a defect of any one candidate): hero blue / enemy red is the TG convention and the owner
  approved it on the old main, but Civil War viewers expect Union = blue. The SHERMAN/CLEBURNE pins carry the meaning; fine as is.

## Feed-size comparison vs TG winners
At 168x94 among LET THEM COME / "AMATEURS" / AT THE GATES, 02 holds up: the word is readable, small-blue-vs-big-red reads at a glance.
Our three are darker and lower-contrast than TG's cold, bright maps (TG maps are pale grey-white so the blocks pop);
the face also sits darker. That is the one gap left to TG.
