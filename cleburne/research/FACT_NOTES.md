## Numbers used in the script (source, confidence)
- Cleburne's division ~4,000 at Tunnel Hill and Ringgold (Wikipedia Missionary Ridge: "about 4,000"; ECW Ringgold: "about 4,000"). OR figure 4,157 for Ringgold is Gemini-only: UNVERIFIED, so "about four thousand".
- Sherman at Tunnel Hill: ~16,000 in three divisions (Wikipedia 16,600; NPS "roughly 16,000"). Gemini's "25,000" is WRONG for the wing. Sherman actually committed far fewer at once.
- Attack started about 08:00, ~90 min late, Corse (~920 men, NPS) and Loomis first; Sherman: "Go back and make that charge immediately; time is everything" (NPS). Matthies mortally wounded, Col. Putnam (93rd Illinois) killed, Raum committed 2 pm (all NPS). Gemini's "Matthies/Raum held in reserve at 11:30, counterattack at 3 pm" conflicts; sources say counterattack ~4 pm.
- Hardee sent reinforcements (NPS). Counterattack ~4 pm along the whole line, Federals "surrendered in large numbers" (NPS).
- Sherman lost almost 2,000 at Tunnel Hill (Wikipedia). Cleburne's loss 222 (secondary mention; OR to confirm): script says "a little over two hundred".
- "the highest point on my line" = Cleburne's report (civilwartalk transcript of the OR report, via search excerpt).
- Cleburne was sent toward Knoxville by train and recalled on 24 Nov (standard; Gemini + report opening; recheck OR).
- Ringgold: Bragg's order verbatim: "to hold this position at all hazards, and keep back the enemy until the artillery and transportation of the army are secure" (ECW/city site). Two guns (Lt. Richard Goldthwaite). Osterhaus later recalled only ~200 cavalry ahead (ECW). Union advanced in seven columns (ECW, Bevens). Casualties: Cleburne 20 killed, 190 wounded, 11 missing = 221 (ECW, city of Ringgold; Wikipedia 221). Union "over 500" (ECW; Wikipedia 509). Union strength "more than ten thousand" (sources range 12,000-16,000: hedge). Grant halted the pursuit (ECW). Union flag planted on the crest then lost: ECW. Creighton and Crane killed: Gemini only, standard history: VERIFY in Geary's report. Thanks of the Confederate Congress: Wikipedia (for the 1863 actions).
- Bragg resigned within days of Ringgold: from memory (asked relief 29 Nov, accepted 2 Dec). VERIFY.
- Pickett's Mill: Union Wood's division 1,457 (212 killed, 927 wounded, 318 missing; ECW), whole Union ~1,565-1,600 (battlefields.org 1,580; Georgia Encyclopedia ~1,600); Confederate 448 (ECW; Cleburne's report). Gemini's 14,000 for Howard's column: UNVERIFIED (not in script, only "a large force"). Confederates had arrived hours earlier and were NOT entrenched (ECW); Gemini's log breastworks WRONG; Bierce, however, says "breastworks constructed at leisure": his claim, not used. Cavalry held the Confederate right (ECW). "General Howard planned it" / "Sherman ordered it" / Wood: "We will put in Hazen and see what success he has" / Hazen "uttered never a word" / "criminal blunder" / "a quarter-mile uphill through almost impassable tangle of underwood": all Bierce (battlefields.org, ECW). Howard wounded in the foot: NOT FOUND, dropped. Night bayonet charge, 172 prisoners: UNVERIFIED, dropped (script: "cleared the ravine and took prisoners" -> CHECK).
- Dalton memorandum, 2 Jan 1864: slavery "one of our chief sources of weakness"; "As between the loss of independence and the loss of slavery ... give up the negro slave rather than be a slave himself" (BOFT, OR Series I vol. 52 pt. 2 pp. 586-92 per Wikipedia). Davis ordered it suppressed (BOFT/Georgia Historical Society). Motive = win the war and save the Confederacy; he never owned slaves (BOFT). Congress authorized Black troops <4 months after his death (BOFT).
- Biography: born Co. Cork 1828; enlisted British 41st Foot 27 Feb 1846 as a private (Wikipedia; "corporal" is "citation needed"), bought discharge 1849; Helena, Arkansas (druggist, then lawyer: memory, VERIFY). Killed at Franklin 30 Nov 1864 leading an assault he opposed (Wikipedia). "Stonewall Jackson of the West": Shelby Foote (Wikipedia); the "Stonewall of the West" origin is unsourced; script says "Later writers".
- "Stakes" for the hook: Bragg's order itself (artillery and transportation to be secured) + Army of Tennessee in retreat. We do NOT say "Atlanta would have fallen six months early" (Gemini analysis, unsourced).
