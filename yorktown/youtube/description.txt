In 1781 the American Revolution was broke, its army unpaid and close to mutiny. The British dug in at Yorktown, Virginia, sure that the Royal Navy would keep them safe. In three weeks, the safe harbor became a trap.

This is the story of Yorktown, told on animated painted maps: Washington and Rochambeau's march south that fooled the British in New York, the Battle of the Capes where de Grasse's French fleet shut the Chesapeake, and the siege, from the first trench dug in the rain to the night storming of Redoubts 9 and 10, Cornwallis's failed escape across the York River in a storm, and the surrender on 19 October 1781.

Which part won Yorktown: the march, the sea, or the siege? Tell us in the comments, and tell us which battle we should tell next.
