Checked at 1280x720, at 168x94 (PIL), next to their references and next to the Yorktown sidebar (`competitor_battle_thumbs.jpg`).

## D2 (Eastory map): APPROVE once fixes 1-3 are made
Scores: feed readability **8**, click appeal vs sidebar **8**, closeness to the Eastory reference **6**.
- At feed size it works: "Yorktown", the red pocket and four arrows closing in still read as "trapped". It is also the only map thumbnail in the sidebar (the rest are AI soldiers, PragerU and PBS paintings), which is what DATA_RULES 3 asks for.
- Defects:
  1. **Fake details (0b item 3, "no fake battle details"):** the NATO "XXXX" army boxes (blue 3/4/2/3/4/3, red 3/2/1/1/2/1) imply about a dozen armies, and 1781 had no armies of that kind. Remove them all, or keep at most 2 blue boxes (US, FR) and 1 red box, with no numbers.
  2. **Modern route shields** "7" (60,490), "9" (540,610) and "15" (920,580) look like highway signs. Delete them.
  3. **"1781 MAPPED" (top left) copies Eastory's "WWII MAPPED" brand.** Use our own brand corner instead (SUMMARY item 1: one channel template), keeping "1781".
  4. **Contrast:** the pale-blue land is almost the same colour as the water, so the blue arrows blend into the land at 168 px. Tint the land sand or green as Eastory does and keep the water blue.
  5. Optional: the east arrow (from the York River and the bay) could be French navy blue or carry a small fleet icon. That makes the sea-battle side visible and helps title C.
- Geography is right: Gloucester north of the river, Hampton to the south-east, Williamsburg to the west, James River to the south.
- Fits titles A and B, and C weakly.

Scores: feed readability **7**, click appeal vs sidebar **5**, closeness to the Epic History reference **6**.
- At 168 px, "YORKTOWN" and "THE TRAP" read well, but the rider's face is about 5 px wide: no face pop.
- In the sidebar it looks like what is already there: a big YORKTOWN title plus an AI Washington-style figure, the same as the "Yorktown AI film" and "Revolution AI" thumbnails.
- Defects:
  1. **The map is invented:** the AI river map is not the York and Chesapeake geography, and the red ellipse (around 490,590) sits on water. Replace it with our real Style W crop (Yorktown on the south bank, Gloucester opposite).
  2. **The bottom arrow (500-580, 650-720) is cut off** by the frame edge. Move it up or delete it.
  3. **The AI rider must not pass as Washington's likeness.** Keep him unnamed, never write "Washington" next to him, and note in FACT_NOTES that he is an illustration. Better: use a real period portrait (Peale or Trumbull) for the face pop.
  4. The rider is too small and the horse takes the centre. Crop tighter, so the face is about 18% of the height, and drop the sword that runs off the top edge.
  5. The empty space in the "1781" bar can go. Add a red accent block like Epic's corner to raise its quality.
- Fits titles A and B ("THE TRAP"), not C.
