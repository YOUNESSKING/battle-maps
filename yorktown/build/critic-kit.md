# Critic: Yorktown kit (round 1)

**VERDICT: NO** (the look is close to the living-map reference, but towns, roads and the Yorktown ferry are missing or too faint, and several labels and units collide).

1. **kit-a (east wide): no painted towns and roads you can barely see** (2e items 1 and 3). New York, Philadelphia and Yorktown have no visible settlement. The post road of the march shows only as a dust smear, while the reference at 2 s has clear ruts and painted towns. Fix: render painted towns at New York (Manhattan tip), Philadelphia (on the Delaware) and Yorktown, and strengthen the rut roads Hudson - King's Ferry - Trenton - Philadelphia - Head of Elk so they read at full frame.
2. **kit-a: the "Philadelphia" label collides with the XX echelon mark and the dust trail** (~890,450: the "i" is covered, so it reads "Philadelph a"). Fix: move it >= 20 px clear, or move the unit box. The label is also nearly straight; set it on an arc beside the town.
3. **kit-b + storm: no Yorktown-Gloucester ferry** (brief: ferry landing + flatboat; 2e item 3). The Gloucester Point road also ends without one. Fix: add the landings and a flatboat at the narrows (storm ~960,420).
4. **kit-storm (16 Oct): the siege has vanished.** The British inner line, Redoubts 9/10, both parallels and every allied unit shown in kit-b on 14 Oct are gone, and the single red box sits on top of the town houses (~900,495). Fix: keep the works and units on screen, dimmed by the night, and move the box off the roofs to the beach/landing.
5. **Labels touch units** (2e item 2, >= 20 px): in kit-b "Yorktown" (~930,420) touches the X mark of the red box at ~905,455 and the British line; it is also straight, not arced. In inset frame 2, the red box sits on top of the "Yorktown" label. Fix: arc the name around the town's north/west edge on open grass and keep units >= 20 px away.
6. **kit-inset-strip: the date scroll is empty in all three frames,** with stray artefacts: a giant carved "O"/"C" letter at left mid-frame (frames 2 and 3, ~x 20,y 280 of each), a blurred dark land band on the left edge of frame 3, and ghost lines behind the scroll in frame 2. Frame 1 is a soft, upscaled blur. Fix: show the date text (or no scroll until the date word), remove the stray label/overlay layer, and bake the close inset at proper resolution.
7. **kit-a: the de Grasse ship box is outside the capes** (~878,1020, in the Atlantic off the Delmarva tip). In Sept 1781 he anchored inside, in Lynnhaven Bay behind Cape Henry. Fix: place it inside the bay mouth (or keep it outside only for Graves's arrival). The "Yorktown" label at the bottom edge (~620,1047) is cropped by the frame; let it step out or move the camera.
8. **kit-b: allied units sit behind the lines, not in them.** On 14 Oct the guns were in the batteries of the first/second parallel; here the blue gun boxes and XX boxes float 50-250 px south of the only allied line. Only one parallel is drawn although the second (11 Oct) existed. There is also no French flag or French distinction on the western (French) half. Fix: put the batteries on the parallels and draw the second parallel joining R9/R10 to the river. Show the French wing west and the Americans east (the French pennant/flag where narration names them).
9. **Banners:** Washington's banner (kit-a ~690,540) covers the head of the Chesapeake (Head of Elk), the key spot of the march; move it to a side stake. Clinton's banner reads grey/maroon, not red cloth, with grey streamers; use the side red. Rochambeau's white fleur-de-lis pennant is almost blank at 1080p and could read as a white flag; enlarge the lilies or give it a gold edge.
10. **kit-b: Cornwallis's banner is jammed at the top edge,** next to the date scroll on the creek mouth (~680,140). Move it inside the town lines, off the frame edge.

Geography passes: Yorktown on the south bank at the narrows with Gloucester Point opposite (storm frame), the York River running NW-SE, the siege lines S/SE of the town, R10 on the river bluff, and New York / Philadelphia / Chesapeake placement on east. No camps, farms, blocks, dashed arrows or modern features seen. Woods, texture and blue carved rivers match the reference.
