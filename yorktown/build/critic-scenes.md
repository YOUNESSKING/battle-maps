# Critic: Yorktown scene sheets (10 scenes)

**Overall: ready to render: NO.** The kit, cards, dates, weather and flags are mostly right. Two problems block the render. First, the owner's one change (front lines in colour) is missing on the 28 Sep - 3 Oct siege ring and in the hook's payoff shot. Second, several banner poles stand in the sea or on the wrong bank. All of these are position or line fixes, not rebuilds.

## hook-a: NO
1. **12.5-21.4 s:** the ring closes with no lines at all: no blue allied half circle and no red British works. The owner asked for allied blue, British red, and a two-colour glow where they face. Fix: draw a red line on the British works and a blue arc from river to river, two-colour where they face, on "became a trap".
2. 21.4 s: the hatching on the town is a grey-blue that barely shows. Fix: use full taker-blue (2c item 2).
3. 7.5 s: no tile shows MUTINY. Confirm the stamp fires on "mutiny" (~6 s).
4. Minor, 15-21 s: the French ships sit out in the bay, not at the river mouth (script: "block the river mouth"). Fix: move 2-3 of them to the York mouth.

## hook-b: YES
1. 21.4 -> 21.6 s (from hook-a): the blue ring and the French ships vanish in one frame before the pull-back. Fix: fade them out over ~0.4 s during the pull.
2. Passes: US flag at R10, redoubts red before the assault, two-colour trench line, sea battle dated 5 SEP with French and British flags, date kept under the title card.

## p1a: NO
1. **104.9-150.8 s:** Clinton's banner pole stands in the Atlantic off Long Island. Fix: plant it on Manhattan.
2. 150.8 s: the THE SEA stamp is a faint ghost and lands as the shot pulls out. Fix: use a cream-on-dark stamp at full strength on the zoomed shot (same for HARBOR in p2a).

## p1b: NO
1. **219-231 s:** Washington's banner is west of the Hudson while his army is still east of it at Dobbs Ferry. Fix: keep it with the east-bank boxes until King's Ferry (~235 s).
2. **271-311 s:** Rochambeau's banner sits on the New Jersey coast or in the water, away from the French column. Fix: pin it to the French column, then to Chester at 5 SEP.
3. 263 s (2 SEP): Washington's banner is already far south of Philadelphia while the column is still there. Fix: move it with the column.
4. 199 s: the A option lights on the first "attack New York" (the "planned all summer" line, ~7 s before the question). Fix: key it to the second "attack New York" (~206 s).
5. 183-214 s: the map hardly moves for ~30 s (rule B3: keep the camera pushing). Fix: add a slow push and let the de Grasse line keep drawing.

## p2a: NO

2. **478-486 s (p2-7, "the door to the sea was shut"):** there is no blue line across the capes, only a faint glow. Fix: draw a blue line from Cape Henry to Cape Charles, with the FORM 1 blue hatching at readable strength.
