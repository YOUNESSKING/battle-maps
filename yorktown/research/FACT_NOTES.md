## Flags (map icons)
US: no single national flag carried at Yorktown (regimental colours); the 13-star flag is used as the generic US symbol (owner's era rule). British: Union Flag 1606 (no St Patrick's cross). French: white with gold fleurs-de-lis (Johnston).

## Weather allowed on the maps
5 Sep clear, NNE breeze (Capes); night 6-7 Oct gentle rain; night 16-17 Oct violent storm of wind and rain (from ~midnight). Everything else: clear.
