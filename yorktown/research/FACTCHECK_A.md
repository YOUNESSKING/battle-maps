# FACTCHECK_A: Gemini Yorktown dossier, claims 1-26

Checked 2026-11-11. Founders Online (founders.archives.gov) is NOT reachable from this sandbox (empty/202 responses), so Washington letters were checked through search snippets plus quotations in Johnston 1881, Stevens (Clinton-Cornwallis Controversy, 1888) and Thacher (1823/1862), whose full texts were read on archive.org (ids: yorktowncampaign00johnrich, campaigninvirgin01/02stevuoft, militaryjournald00thac). "J" = Johnston, "S" = Stevens, "T" = Thacher. Where only a secondary source was reachable it says so.

Verdicts: C = CONFIRMED, FIX = CORRECTED, TRAD = TRADITION, UNV = UNVERIFIED.

## Cross-cutting notes
- All figures marked "J" came from Johnston (1881), who quotes primary letters. The exact Clinton 24 Sep and Cornwallis 8 Jul/20 Oct wordings above are direct Stevens transcriptions of the originals and are safe to quote.
- Items I could not source and Gemini should not be trusted on: "$20,000" Morris loan, "840 seamen", "unpaid X months" for the Pennsylvania Line, "3 officers killed", 20 July letter to O'Hara, French siege loss ~250, 4 AM sortie.
