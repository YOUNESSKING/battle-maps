## Description

In January 1781, on an open cattle pasture in South Carolina, a crippled frontier wagon driver wiped out the most feared legion in the British Army in less than an hour. This is the story of Daniel Morgan, the Old Wagoner: the riflemen of Saratoga, the perfect trap at Cowpens, and the blueprint that sent Cornwallis toward Yorktown.
