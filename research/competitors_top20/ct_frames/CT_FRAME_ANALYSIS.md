## 2. Their craft (repeating devices, all verified in the shot lists/transcripts)
- **Scale ladder in every move:** strategic map -> regional satellite map -> tactical close-up -> archive/painting of the result.
- **Visual metaphors on the map:** Model's "checkerboard" defence as a lattice of trenches + diamond strongpoints; funnels and kill zones; fog question marks over hidden French divisions (Austerlitz). **Props:** mines, wire, anti-tank obstacles, crossed sabres; photo insets of the real object (canvas assault boat 16:39, Jagdpanther 17:39).
- **Numbers:** troop counters that add up on screen (38,000 vs 25,000 + 30,000 -> 55,000; the sum is shown, not spoken); a total turned into a vivid rate ("almost a thousand every single hour").
- **Set-up and pay-off:** Eisenhower's warning (12:46) paid off later ("as Eisenhower predicted...", 16:31).
- **Human detail mid-move:** ~30 s on Model scouting by plane and moving MG positions himself (4:47-5:19); the Tafelberg Hotel moment (14:30).
- **Honesty hedges:** "likely exaggerated for propaganda" (Austerlitz ice), "some argue Stalin held off reserves" (10:48).
- **First-person narrator:** "There are many battles I could have begun with, but I chose Montenotte because..."; "which commander should I cover next?"
- **People and archive:** colourised cut-out portraits slide in when named; a speech bubble with a quote ("Monty, you're nuts!"); Frost's last message as text; archive picture-in-picture in rounded red/blue glowing frames over the map sector (Model, a block every ~1-2 min, 10-40 s each); Napoleon cuts to a painting or a film clip every ~10-30 s (feature-film clips: Napoleon 2023, Waterloo 1970), and once to modern-day location footage (Pratzen Heights today).
- **Sound (Napoleon):** a distinct effect for each map action (musket on feints, sword clang, water on rivers, metal clang when roads close, ice cracking) and music changing per beat. Model is sparse (artillery rumble).
- **Pace:** ~153-157 wpm when speaking, about the same as ours (~159-176 measured), with music-only beats of 5-23 s (cold open, each card, outro).
