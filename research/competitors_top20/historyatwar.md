## Titles

2021: 'WW2: <Battle> (Intense/Brutal/Combat Footage)', 18-45 characters, search-style. 2024-26: '<X> Was Way Worse Than You Think (UNCENSORED FOOTAGE)', 'When the <A> First Faced the <B>', 'Men of Blood: What the Waffen SS Really Were | Full Story Explained'. Recurrent bracket tags: (UNCENSORED FOOTAGE), (NO AI), | World War II, (Full Story). Superlative / reveal phrasing 'Way Worse Than You Think' is repeated 8+ times and still works (700-960K). Weak titles name a narrow story with no promise ('Battle of Castle Itter Explained', 'Why the British Respected German Soldiers').

## Format

Prokhorovka (53:30): 58 s of music/no narration, then a chronological battle narration with NO stakes line, name of the battle only at ~1:31; sections by day (July 12, July 13, Operation Roland), quoted first-person accounts, losses figures at 46-50 min, 'who really won' analysis at 51:00, outro with Patreon at 52:37; narration is a text-to-speech voice (many mis-transcribed names), long sentences, flat; visuals = colourised archive footage plus a few animated maps. BLog0 (Normandy, 40:35, 959K views, 17.9x median): cold open at 0:19 in a person's POV (Bayerlein watching the sky go black; 'Hell is not as bad as what we experienced'), stakes by 1:54 ('through the gap 120,000 American soldiers were about to pour'), then a 'why I have never covered this' aside at 2:09 and a promise 'the story of those 10 days from both sides' at 2:57; chapters = Cobra bombing, Roncey pocket (Langanke), Mortain/Hill 314 (Weiss), epilogue at 31:56 with each man's fate, then ~6 min of repeated material (padding). Quotes from named witnesses carry the pace. Typical length 40-80 min (avg 105 min in last 30 days incl. compilations), cadence 1-2 per week, no mid-roll ask heard in transcripts.

## What works here

- Cold open in one named person's POV at the worst moment, stakes ('120,000 men about to pour through') within 2 minutes.
- Quoting named first-person witnesses chapter by chapter; each chapter is one person.
- Repeatable 'X Was Way Worse Than You Think' title wrapper; the repeat did not exhaust it.

## What fails here

- Narrow-audience topics and 'Why the British respected...' style essays: 5-15K.
- Opening with 58 s of music and no stakes (Prokhorovka) - still 1.3M because of search demand, but retention risk.
- Insignia-heavy thumbnails (skulls, SS runes) and long padded endings; 5 h compilations.

## 3 things OUR channel can copy or must avoid

1. Copy the Normandy cold open pattern: one named witness, one quote, one number of what was about to be lost, topic named by 2:00.
2. Series wrapper in the title that signals revision of common belief ('Way Worse Than You Think') as one of the 3 title variants for Test & Compare; keep claims sourced.
3. Avoid: SS/Nazi insignia in thumbnails, text-to-speech flat reading, and 6 min of repetition after the ending.
