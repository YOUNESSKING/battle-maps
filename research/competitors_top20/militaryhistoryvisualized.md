## Format
Transcripts: Panzerfaust (7:25, 4.1M) and German Squad Tactics in WW2 (12:02, 3.3M).
- **Hook:** the question stated in the first sentence + the plan: "...the question arises: how effective was it? Let's look at some background first, and then tackle the question with some numbers." Squad Tactics: "Time to take a look at German squad tactics ... Two important points". Fast, no music intro, no sponsor in those videos, topic at 0:01.
- **Structure:** specification (numbers: 8.3 million produced, 6.5 kg, 200 mm penetration), mechanism (hollow charge, recoilless), then data and an honest verdict ("take all these numbers with at least two salt mines"; 264 kills of 520 = 50.8 %); source/caveat language in the middle; end: conclusion + patrons + "if you like this, watch my X video".
- **Narration:** one-man, ~130-160 wpm (estimate), dry, careful, German terms spelled out; visuals = diagrams, charts with numbers, own illustrations, footage; strict source discipline ("all sources are linked in the description").
- **Length/cadence:** every 2 weeks (Tuesday 18-19 UTC); typical 10-17 min, a few 20-25 min; winners 3-16 min.

## What works here
- A single, answerable verdict question ("How effective was it?", "Why didn't ...").
- Numbers and a clear verdict; honesty about sources builds a loyal audience.
- Weapons/tactics evergreen search demand (Panzerfaust, Flak 88, Tiger, StuG).

## What fails here
- Recent 2026 StuG/Panzer series: 17-170k (median 79k), series fatigue, host-in-frame thumbnails, vlogs (17-20k).
- Channel peaked in 2017-2019; recent videos are ~0.1-0.3x the old winners.
