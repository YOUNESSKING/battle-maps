## 5. Format

Transcripts: Hattin 1187 (28:10, 5.4M) and Agincourt (33:46, 'ALL PARTS', 3.9M).
- **Hook**: both are 'documentary-first' not 'stakes-first'. Hattin opens on a Saladin quote (0:02) and then spends ~14 minutes (0:20-14:40) on Saladin's biography and the political build-up before the campaign starts at ~15:40; the battle march is 18:26-25:19 (7 min, ~25 % of the video). Agincourt has a 14-s atmospheric cold open (peaceful Harfleur morning, then the invasion fleet appears), but the title battle only starts at ~22:00 (65 %): the first 13 min is the Harfleur siege and the march; a 30-s teaser ("close to noon... not far from the castle of Agincourt... every man kissed the ground") at ~14:00 is the part-2 re-hook. Not a pattern we should copy for a 16-min video, but note both open with the NAME of the subject within 20 s.
- **Structure**: parts with black music-only title cards (gaps in the transcript at 0:48, 5:26, 12:36, 13:32) = the 'ALL PARTS' compilations are 2-5 earlier episodes stitched together. Chronological narrative with maps showing each move, a numbers/debate section on army sizes (Anne Curry cited, 'sources below'), and an end summary of casualties and consequences; closing line like 'that is a story for another time'. No subscribe asks in the narration.
- **Narration**: one calm, measured male voice (~150-165 wpm estimated), heavy on proper names and dates; no jokes; music bed.
- **Visuals**: animated 2D map with unit blocks and arrows, painted/miniature figures, archive paintings, siege diagrams; all produced in-house.
- **Length / cadence**: single battles 18-35 min, series parts 20-45 min, 'ALL PARTS' compilations 1-2 h; one video every Saturday ~16:00-17:30 UTC; median recent views 343k; one 8x outlier in the last 50.

## 6. What works here

1. Closest analogue to us: animated map as the product AND as the thumbnail (map frame + arrow + commander + 1-3 words).
2. Famous battle + stakes clause; recent titles add a counter-intuitive claim ('How One Rifle Destroyed...', 'Forget Vienna').
3. Battle name + year always visible in title; logo on every thumbnail for consistency.
4. Weekly fixed slot.


## 7. What fails here

- 14-minute biography/background before the battle (Hattin) works only because Hattin is a megatopic; unknown battles at 0.4-0.5x.
- 'Part N' and 8-10 word thumbnail captions on obscure battles.


## 8. Three things OUR channel can copy or must avoid

1. Thumbnail test: our own map frame with ONE big arrow + the general's portrait + 1-3 caps words (battle/place + year, e.g. 'HATTIN, 1187'), logo corner. HistoryMarche's best 8 have mean 3.9 words and 75 % maps.
2. Title formula to test as variant B/C: `<counter-intuitive claim about the move>: <General>, <Battle year>` (Koniggratz 'How One Rifle Destroyed the Austrian Empire' = 6.6x vs the same channel's neutral titles at 0.4-0.7x). Keep every claim true and sourced.
3. Avoid 'Part N'/'ALL PARTS' wording and avoid long background openings; HistoryMarche proves the hours-long compilations draw clicks but also shows their 20-35 min singles only hit when the battle is famous: lead our Top-3 with the famous move.
