## Format
Transcripts: Six-Day War (15:59, 12.6M) and Fall of Constantinople (12:02, 4.1M). I skipped the 2-hour Rome/Greece video (#1) to save context, so long-form pacing is not verified.
- **Hook:** none in the modern sense. Six-Day War starts with a 0:10 Skillshare sponsor read, then "Ever since Israeli victory during the wars of 1948 and 1956..." = background-first. Constantinople opens with "The Ottoman Empire attempted to besiege Constantinople ... six occasions": context, no stakes line, no cold open. Topic named in sentence 1-2 (it is the title).
- **Structure:** chronological. Causes -> forces (numbers: 240k vs 100k troops, 2504 vs 800 tanks) -> front by front (Sinai, Jordan, Syria) -> casualties -> consequence. No chapter cards, no recap lines, few emotional beats. Ends: casualties/result, one-line legacy, then sponsor read + subscribe/Patreon + "This is the Kings and Generals channel, and we will catch you on the next one."
- **Narration:** male calm documentary voice, ~140-150 wpm (estimate), dense with names, dates, unit numbers. Visuals: their animated maps with unit icons, arrows, flags, plus paintings/archive stills.
- **Monetisation:** sponsor spot at 0:10 (pre-roll style) and at the end (14:40); Patreon credit.
- **Length/cadence:** a long video roughly every 2 days; modern 17-25 min typical (avg long ~30 min in 30 d), with periodic 2-6 h "All battles explained" compilations (some do 0.5-1.0M, War of the Spanish Succession 1.0M). Winners: era = Rome (6 of 10), early Islam, Hundred Years' War, Arab-Israeli wars; sides = no loyalty angle.

## What works here
- Whole-war topics people already search (Rome, Caesar, Mongols, Crusades, Arab-Israeli wars); the big views come from long-lived search/suggested traffic on those evergreen subjects over 4-8 years.
- A thumbnail that is title-in-big-letters over an illustrated or map background, readable at feed size.
- Very high output; 30-90 min deep dives are tolerated by the audience.

## What fails here
- Non-war/generic social-history subjects (Cyrus, medieval horror, Romani) land at 0.06-0.2M, against a 0.33M median.
- Recent 2026 uploads of 0.2-0.7M are far below the old winners; nothing recent reaches 1M except a 4h50 compilation.
- Torn-paper "stories" template + 8-word thumbnails.

1. Thumbnail structure: one illustrated focal image (general or two rivals in a versus layout) over a faded map + a 3-5 word caps headline lower third. Matches our "<General>'s Top 3 Legendary Tactical Moves" thumbnail brief.
