Transcripts: Iew9cu (31:01) opens at 0:04 with 'Good day everybody and welcome back to the channel', explains in 30 s the concept (first gun camera breakdown) and names the topic at 0:35; then 25 min of clip-by-clip commentary (pace ~165 wpm estimate, casual, swearing, jokes, aviation explanations, self-plug 'plugging my next video' at 12:59). No chapters, no music beds heard, ends with plain thanks. Visuals are footage + screenshots + memes, no maps/animation. Typical video 1h to 3h+; cadence roughly monthly (last 50 uploads cover 3.7 years).

## What works here

- Recognisable series banner on thumbnails; each series entry builds on the previous one (Horrible History of Russian Fighter Jets x6).
- Personality-led voice with jokes keeps people through 1-2 hours.
- Reacting to big news within days (B-17 collision, Ukraine bomber raid) produced the 2.3M and 380K videos.

## What fails here

- Off-brand topics (anime ship girls, Apollo 11 recovery, generic naval tales) get 3-17% of the median.
- Very long runtimes (3-6 h sleep compilations) do not add views: 100-160K.
- Median 229K is carried by series and personality; nothing here is a repeatable pure-war-story format.

## 3 things OUR channel can copy or must avoid

1. Series banner template on thumbnails (a fixed corner/ribbon for 'Top 3 Legendary Tactical Moves') for recognition: keep one stable frame and swap only the face and 1-3 words.
2. If we publish a news-linked anniversary battle, release within days of the anniversary/news peak: the biggest hit here was timing, not craft.
3. Avoid: heavy comedic/profanity voice, 60-min length and personality dependence; our faceless AI voice cannot carry that.
