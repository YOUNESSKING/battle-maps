## 5. Format

Transcripts: Golan Heights (46:25, 7.7M) and 73 Easting (46:23, 5.1M); both are licensed TV episodes ('Greatest Tank Battles').
- **Hook**: classic TV cold open: 0-67 s of soundbites over action: 'a few hundred unprepared Israeli tankers come face to face with 12,000 Syrian tanks', 'It's inconceivable. It's an inferno', then the series opening restarts at 1:23 with the full narrator intro. 73 Easting: 0:00-0:52 'bleak and forbidding desert... two tank armies clash... This is the Battle of 73 Easting... probably the last great tank battle of the 20th century'. Subject named within 20 s; the stakes (numbers, 'last great tank battle') come with the name.
- **Structure**: 4 acts of ~11-12 minutes, each ending on a cliffhanger and each starting with a 60-90 s recap of the previous act (Golan recaps at 15:41, 26:47, 39:47; 73 Easting at 14:20, 24:53, 33:52, 41:42) = the TV ad-break pattern; on YouTube these are the natural mid-roll points. Background is ~5-9 minutes (Saddam, GPS, 7th Corps) before first contact at 13:47 (73 Easting) while Golan is in the attack by 2:00. Mix of first-person tank-crew interviews (some translated), expert historians (talking heads) and narrator; ends on casualties ("21,000 dead", "one killed and 12 wounded"), the consequence (Republican Guard survived, 2003 war) and a quote.
- **Narration**: authoritative US-TV narrator, ~140-160 wpm effective, with heavy ambient battle sound and music; no calls to subscribe inside the programme.
- **Visuals**: archive film, reconstructions, CGI tank diagrams, interview shots, some animated maps (not map-driven).
- **Length / cadence**: TV episodes 44-52 min; 2-3.9 h compilations ('complete story', 'in numbers'); recent upload rhythm 1-2 per day at ~19:00-21:00 UTC; median recent views ~50k.

## 6. What works here

1. Cliffhanger-plus-recap act structure every ~11 min (retention + mid-roll friendly).
2. Cold open that states the odds in numbers in the first 20 s ('a few hundred tankers vs 12,000 tanks').
3. One emotive colourised face or scene, text-free thumbnail, brand logo bottom-left, one saturated accent colour.
4. 'Uncensored / Rare footage / Complete story' title words drive the 2024-26 hits.


## 7. What fails here

- Mass daily output of low-interest topics (9-12k views = 0.2x).
- 5-9 minutes of background before the battle even in 46-min TV programmes.
- 3-hour compilation 'overview' videos are not our product.


## 8. Three things OUR channel can copy or must avoid

1. End each of the 3 moves on a cliffhanger line and open the next with a one-sentence recap; fits our casualty/result card and keeps retention through moves 1-2 (DATA RULES: minutes watched).
2. Put the odds in the first spoken sentence of the hook with numbers ('a few hundred vs 12,000') as Golan Heights does.
3. For thumbnails test a single emotive face/portrait with one saturated accent colour and <=3 words; the 'Uncensored footage' formula is NOT available to a faceless animated-map channel, avoid chasing it.
