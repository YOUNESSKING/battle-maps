## 5. Format

Not applicable (feature films, no narration). Length 1h-2h25 (typical 1h25); cadence: 1 upload every ~4 weeks (Saturday 10:45 UTC: 4 Jan, 25 Jan, 19 Apr, 17 May, 13 Jun, 11 Jul, 15 Aug, 12 Sep, 10 Oct, 7 Nov 2026); the 2017-19 backlog of ~25 public-domain films gives most of the 1.15M-sub base. Recent releases: ~10k-400k, last (1 day old) 1.2k.

## 6. What works here

1. Search keyword stacking ('Full Length War Movie English HD') on a stable template.
2. A fixed upload slot (every 4th Saturday).


## 7. What fails here

- Recent films fall to 10-35k as the free public-domain library runs dry.
- Text-free, logo-free thumbnails for unknown titles.


## 8. Three things OUR channel can copy or must avoid

1. Nothing to copy structurally: it is a film-licensing channel. Keep our own format.
2. Avoid keyword-stuffed 'Full Length ... HD 720p' style titles and any film-still imagery (copyright risk).
3. Only lesson: a fixed upload slot helps viewers/algorithm; do not let our cadence drift.
