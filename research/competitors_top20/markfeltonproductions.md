## Format
Transcripts: At the Gates of Moscow (10:04, 8.2M) and Reichstag Assault 1945 (10:17, 8.2M).
- **Hook:** none or minimal; Moscow: music, then "22nd of June 1941 Hitler unleashed Operation Barbarossa, the largest invasion in history" = date + big claim, 0-17 s. Reichstag: opens with a context sentence, "The Soviets viewed the Reichstag building in the same way that they viewed the Kremlin" and goes straight into forces. Topic is named by sentence 1.
- **Structure:** strictly chronological campaign with dates, place names and unit numbers; ends with a **fun-fact twist** (Moscow: "Khimki, the Germans saw the Kremlin's domes? ... camouflage"; Reichstag: the faked flag-raising reenactment, the building's scars today), then "thanks for watching please subscribe and share" + cross-promo of his second channel + PayPal/Patreon. No sponsor in these two.
- **Narration:** Mark Felton himself (personal anecdote: "I accidentally visited Khimki some years ago when I missed a connecting flight") ~125-150 wpm (estimate), measured British delivery. Visuals: archival footage and photographs, colourised film; maps sparse.
- **Length/cadence:** a video every 3-4 days; 7-15 min typical (avg long 12.6 min), occasional 26-39 min "Complete Series". Winners (8-12M) = 4-22 min. Era: WW2 end phase; side: German/Nazi leadership topics dominate the top.

- People-plus-secret topics about the Third Reich's end (wives, voices, bodies, bunker) get 8-12M; battles at 8M.
