## Format
Transcripts: Opis Mutiny speech (7:39, 16.1M) and Waterloo 1815 (13:57, 6.3M).
- **Hook:** Opis = no intro at all: starts in the middle of Alexander's speech ("What I'm about to say isn't meant to stop you returning home...") read as a dramatic monologue; the whole video IS the hook. Waterloo = a 4-sentence context cold open: "April 1814 for 10 years one man has dominated Europe: Napoleon" - person and stakes named within 5 s, then Elba, return, the Coalition declares him an outlaw; the battle starts at ~5:00.
- **Structure:** story arc with causality ("Napoleon decides to act", "Wellington has chosen his battlefield with care"); time stamps in the narration (11 a.m., 1 p.m., 4 p.m., 7:30 p.m.); a closing epilogue (Wellington's quote, casualties, Elba-> St Helena, then photographs of the real veterans). Ends on a human image, no subscribe ask in the narration; a Patreon thank-you line (Opis) at the end.
- **Narration:** professional male narrator, ~120-130 wpm (estimate), plain short sentences, vivid verbs ("smashing bloody holes in the Allied ranks"), few numbers. Visuals: 3D animated maps, 3D units, paintings, cinematic reconstructions; a distinct high production value.
- **Monetisation:** no sponsor read in these videos (Patreon credit only).
- **Length/cadence:** one big video every ~1-3 weeks (recent list: Oct 22, Sep 30, Sep 3, Aug 20), 20-38 min typical, plus "(All Parts)" compilations 1-5 h. The best sellers are compilations of a series (Alexander 29M, WWI 24M/16.6M, Downfall 16M).

## What works here
- A series in chapters, then "(All Parts)" compilations with a new thumbnail: 7 of the top 10 are compilations/long.
- A superlative question title + dramatic dialogue (Opis speech 16M from a 7-minute video).
- Immediately recognisable thumbnail brand; face + big single word.
- Storytelling with causality and an emotional closing image.

## What fails here
- Generic, non-battle explainers (5 Ancient Mysteries 0.14M, Eagles in the Snow short film 0.15M).
- Very low cadence of new videos in 2026 (about one per 3-4 weeks) while the back-catalogue carries the channel.

## 3 things OUR channel can copy or must avoid
1. Open on the story's key moment or the central quote, no preamble: Opis got 16M from a 7:39 monologue. Fits our "stakes then zoom" hook.
2. Superlative question title: "The Greatest ...?" paired with a general; test for the Top-3 packaging (title option B).
3. AVOID: the (All Parts) compilation dependency; also a thumbnail with 12 words (Tsarist Russia).
