## Findings
**What works here (winners):** one repeatable template: animated map with the big place name as the ONLY text (Berlin, Moscow, Kursk, Stalingrad, Dunkirk), red/blue arrows, red front line, unit boxes, small channel logo top-left, thin coloured frame. 7 of 8 winners are maps, 0 have faces. The four biggest are the Eastern Front year series (1941 to 1944/45), 10M each; the topic (WW2 Eastern Front, Stalingrad/Moscow/Berlin) carries them. Title formula: '<Theatre> animated: <year>' (31-35 characters, no hook words, no caps) and 'WW2 - <theatre>, <years>'.
**What fails:** off-theme explainers and leaders (LOSERS? with four portraits, 37k = 0.2x), later series parts (Russian Civil War IV, 134k) and obscure theatres (Spain 172k). Thumbnails got MORE cluttered over time (arrows, years, taglines) while views fell; but older winners are 5-8 years old, so the recency gap is mostly accumulated views.
**Typical length / cadence:** 9-18 min; about 1 video per month now (61 long videos total; Dec 2024 - Nov 2025 = 7 Russian Civil War parts).
## Format (transcripts of the #1 and #2, Eastern Front 1944/45 and 1941)
- **Hook:** none in the dramatic sense. Both open at t=0:00-0:07 with a flat date + situation sentence ('Europe, summer 1941. Germany had secured most of Central and Western Europe...'; 'Early summer 1944. The war had decisively turned against Germany...'). Stakes are strategic (the plan), not human; no cold open, no number, no question. Topic named in sentence 1.
- **Structure:** a campaign told sector by sector ('First we'll look at the southern sector', 'Meanwhile, army group north...'), with a plan-then-result loop (Soviet plan: encircle Army Group Centre; result: encircled east of Minsk) and a recurring 'two options, they chose the latter' decision beat. No chapters, no recap lines, no named heroes: armies and army groups are the characters.
- **Narration:** calm, neutral, ~160 words per minute (1941: ~1,500 words in 9:20; 1944/45: ~2,700 in 16:50); non-native British English; no music cue text, quiet background music (it is a map-with-pen animation with unit boxes).
- **Calls to action:** none in the middle; the 1944/45 outro (15:55-16:49) thanks Patrons and sells Kindle Unlimited with three book recommendations (affiliate links in description); no mid-roll ask.
- **Ending:** a sentence that sets up the next episode ('1942 would be the decisive year of the war'), series-finale line.
## 3 things for OUR channel
1. **Copy:** the single big place name as the only on-map thumbnail text; one red front line + arrows = readable at feed size. We already do maps; ensure the thumbnail shows ONE named place, not a whole theatre.
2. **Copy:** the plan -> result -> decision-beat loop (what the general intended, what happened, the alternative he declined) to keep a map-only narration moving without hero drama.
3. **Avoid:** their flat opening ('Europe, summer 1941...') and a cast of armies; our hook rule (stakes + a human) is the differentiator. Also avoid series-part numbering in titles ('RCW IV') which dropped to 0.7x.
