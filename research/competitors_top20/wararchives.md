## Titles

Literal description + year in brackets + '| War Archives': 'Hitler's Bunker Revealed by the British (1945)', 'Gestapo Torture Chamber Found Under Eiffel Tower (1944)', 'Cameraman Accidentally Killed In Explosion on US Aircraft Carrier (1953)'. Shock verbs (Executed, Burnt Alive, Killed, Shot Down, Revealed), named person (Hitler, FDR) and a year. 45-90 chars, no caps tricks. Weak titles are neutral ('Life on The Falklands (1960s)', 'Christmas Celebrations in Tobruk (1943)').

## Format

No transcript pulled: these are 22 s - 9 min British Pathe newsreel clips with original commentary or silent footage; no hook, no host, no chapters. Cadence: weekly in 2011-2015, then stopped. Best lesson is only about search demand: shocking, specific, dated historical footage with people on screen can pull millions via search/suggested over many years.

## What works here

- Clear literal title with year and a shock word, matched exactly by the thumbnail text.
- People in extreme situations on screen (face close-up) beat equipment/benign scenes by 10x.
- Evergreen long tail (2011 uploads still earn).

## What fails here

- Benign everyday newsreels (parties, stunts, wildlife) at 6-13K.
- Template never changes - no feed differentiation, channel is dormant.

## 3 things OUR channel can copy or must avoid

1. Use a year-in-brackets + specific result in archive-block labels and in one of the title variants (e.g. 'Rokossovsky at Bagration (1944)') for search matching.
2. Our archive photo inserts should always show a face in distress/focus rather than equipment; face close-ups beat scenery.
3. Avoid: copying the channel at all as a format model (dormant, rights-based reposts); do not use Pathe/newsreel clips without licence.
