## Findings
**Flag:** faceless slideshow channel; topic = Nazi/Axis criminals, executions and victim stories. Not battles. The only 'war' video with soldiers is the Dachau reprisals one. Median of the recent 10 is 179k.
**What works here (winners):** an identical 2-panel template for every video: colourised or b/w archive photo on the left (face, usually a woman) and a second photo/portrait on the right split by an angled edge; the channel mark in the top-left; NO words at all. Faces in 8/8 winners, text on 0/8, no maps. The title carries everything: 'Final Reckoning of Auschwitz's Most Feared Nazi Guard: Irma Grese' (5.0M), 'Execution of ...', 'Painful execution of ...', 'The Jew who survived Auschwitz because a Nazi guard fell in love with her' (3.4M); 70-110 characters, a named person after a colon or dash, strong emotive adjectives (Ruthless, Painful, Brutal, Sadistic), 'How/When ... Paid'.
**What fails (weak recent):** same thumbnail template on lesser-known figures: 22-46k (0.1-0.3x). So the template is neutral; the NAME and the shock premise drive CTR, and the algorithm is currently splitting a 15-min video per 2 days across too many obscure subjects (recent titles shifted to 'When X Paid For What They Did' formula; the best recent are collective ('The Final Reckoning: How Norway Dealt with Nazi Collaborators' 378k, 2.1x)).
**Length/cadence:** 14-17 min, every 2 days (avg 15.7 min).
## Format (transcripts: Irma Grese 21:31; Dachau Massacre 14:16)
- **Hook:** both open at 0:00 with a dated, concrete, shocking scene: 'The 15th of April, 1945, southwest of Bergen... British 11th Armored Division liberates Bergen-Belsen... 13,000 unburied bodies', and 'Sunday, the 29th of April 1945... 45th Infantry Division liberates Dachau'. Stakes-first, then 'the brutal response is to come' (Dachau, at 0:40). The person/event is named by 0:40-1:00.
- **Structure:** after the cold open the narration rewinds to birth/background (Grese: born 1923, schooling at ~0:58; Dachau: 1933 history at 0:54), 8-10 min of context, then the crime events, then trial/execution with date and 'final word'. The background-heavy middle is exactly what our OPENING RULE forbids; they get away with it because the thumbnail/title already sold the payoff.
- **Narration:** ~155 wpm, TTS-like neutral voice, wiki-style prose with quoted testimonies; background music; slideshow of colourised photos (no maps, no animation).
- **Asks:** only at the end: 'Thanks for watching the World History Channel be sure to like and subscribe and click the Bell...' (20:42-21:17 / 13:56). Nothing mid-roll. Ending: the sentence 'There were no tears shed for Irma Grese'.
