## Titles

'The Most [superlative] ...' (Most Terrifying Man of the Vietnam War, Most Hard Core American Sniper, Nastiest Soldiers, Fastest Flight, Filthiest Chernobyl Men) or 'The Insane Move Germany Never Expected America to Make' / 'The Mystery of the Other Atomic Bomb' (open loop, no number). Short: 7-12 words. 2019-2020 titles add ' - descriptor' (Operation X - what it was). Numbers only when they are the shock (800 Marines vs 2,500 Japanese). Weak titles are longer topic labels with no superlative ('The Tactical Disaster that 10 Presidents Haven't Been Able to Solve', 'The Brutal Reality of Being a WWII Tank Commander'). Recent hits keep 'The Man Who/Too ...' ('The Man Who Taught America True Violence', 'The General Who Answered To Nobody').

## Format

Transcripts (Shriver 12:58, Hathcock 12:51): cold open IN ACTION at 0:00 (a firefight; a sniper duel), the hero is named within 15 s (Shriver) or at 0:00 (Hathcock), a cliffhanger line closes the cold open ('what happened next only cemented ... a Marine Corps legend...'), then a LOGO sting at ~1:00 and straight into birth/bio (born in De Funiak Springs at 1:09; Hathcock childhood at 2:28) - so it breaks OUR 'no CV before 2 min' rule, but only after a 60 s action hook. Then 3-5 short chapters (a 'Chapter 5 Last Fight' header appears at 10:22), one signature quote repeated (the 'surrounded from the inside' line is said at 0:51 and again at 9:07), ending on the death/legacy + medals list. ~155-165 words per minute (estimate), flat documentary voice, colourised photos/archive + AI illustrations, minimal text; no mid-roll ask, one outro line ('subscribe to all our Dark Documentaries channels') at 12:23 and a 'tell us in the comments'. Typical length now 20-30 min (was 10-14); cadence about 1 per week (10 uploads in the last 9 weeks).

## What works here

- Cold open with the peak moment, hero named in <15 s, one repeated quote as the spine.
- One extreme-character promise in the title ('Most/Too ... for ...'), every video built on one person or one raid.
- Thumbnail = ONE big face or ONE mysterious detail with a red arrow/circle, zero words.
- Long tail: 2019-2022 videos still sit at 2-9M views; evergreen single-subject stories compound.

## What fails here

- Abstract / policy-style titles ('Tactical Disaster that 10 Presidents ...', 'Strait of Hormuz Problem') lost to 10-20K views.
- Calm portrait thumbnails with no hook element or odd detail.
- Going 20-30 min without a stronger hook lowered the baseline to ~100K median (10-14 min versions earned 2-9M).

## 3 things OUR channel can copy or must avoid

1. Name the hero by 0:15 and repeat one signature line twice (hook and the climactic moment): matches our 'move 1 by 1:30' rule and can sit in the hook of each Top-3 video.
2. For thumbnails use a SINGLE strong element (face or arrow-circled detail) and no words; our rule 'face pop + one context image + 1-3 words' should be tested in a no-text variant too (Test & Compare).
3. Titles: superlative character label ('The Most ...', 'The Man Who ...') beats topic labels; avoid abstract titles. Avoid: relying on the old 10-14 min length argument - their winners were short, ours are 16 min.
