## What works here

- A promise + open loop in the first 50 s ('find out who or what we found inside') and a thumbnail/title built on 'unseen footage' or a 'German view' angle.
- Footage-location investigations (where exactly was this shot) - the two 2M videos are both.
- Strict, repeatable thumbnail template (bottom bar, caps, one red word) = brand recognition.

## What fails here

- Niche campaign (Gettysburg Civil War, Somme) and 'No AI' tagged videos at 8-30K: topic outside the audience's core.
- Generic dramatic phrases ('IT WAS A MASSACRE') without a specific question.
- Maps with arrows on thumbnails did not lift views here.
