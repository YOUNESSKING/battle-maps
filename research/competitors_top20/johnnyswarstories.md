## Findings
**What works here:** the recognisable film scene itself (Downfall/Hitler, Band of Brothers, Saving Private Ryan, Forrest Gump) with a literal 'Scene - Film' title; very short length (1-3 min; the top video is 56 s); faces in close-up in 6 of 8 winners; no thumbnail text at all, only a 'HD / Johnny's War Stories' logo bottom-left.
**What fails:** unknown/new films (Hover Tank Test, Counterfeiters, Cromwell) get 3-4k views; recent uploads are ~6k median, i.e. the whole channel is ~1000x weaker than its Jan-2021 clips. Outlier scores here are meaningless (median 5.9k vs old viral clips), so the table is only a ranking. Back-catalogue views come from film-title search, which we cannot use.
**Title pattern:** '<Scene description / quote> - <Film>' (30-55 characters), no caps, no numbers, quotes as titles ('You are all war-criminals!'). No curiosity gap, pure search/recognition.
**Format:** no narration; no hook; film clip with channel logo. We did not pull a transcript (clips contain movie dialogue only; the one transcript attempt on D-Day from the Axis Perspective returned only fragments of dialogue).
**Cadence:** about weekly (Oct 2025 - Oct 2026: ~45 uploads); 5 min average.
## 3 things for OUR channel
1. **Copy:** nothing structural; the one useful lesson is that a close-up face on a 16:9 still with NO text still wins when the subject is instantly recognisable. Our general portraits should be big enough to recognise at feed size.
2. **Avoid:** clip/reupload economics (not our format, copyright-fragile).
3. **Avoid:** judging with outlier scores on old videos; compare only like-aged videos (our DATA_RULES already say this).
