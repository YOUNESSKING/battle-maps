- **Browse audiences get "seeded".** 85-97 % of our views come from Home/browse. YouTube pushes a new upload first to people who watched our winners, and those people came for 18th/19th-century American wars. Smith, Gavin and Collins are both off-audience AND obscure names, so we cannot yet tell which of the two killed them. **A famous WWII name (Patton) separates the two causes in one upload**; a mid-fame one (Model, Ridgway) does not.
- **Competitor "x median" figures are confounded.** (a) Channel size: TG has 11.6k subs, FH 2.5k, C&T 36.7k, and a 2x on C&T is worth more raw views than 15x on ours. (b) Age: views are cumulative, so compare v/d. (c) **Single-outlier algorithm pushes**: TG's Ridgway 1.36M and Ravelin's Greene 1.12M are one-off browse explosions; the same subject on the next channel does 1.5x (FH Ridgway). Copying a subject does not copy the explosion. (d) **First-mover vs follower**: FH's late copies of saturated names landed at 0.7-1.8x (Zhukov 0.85, Rommel 0.7, Rokossovsky 1.0, Ridgway 1.5, Grant 1.8), while its uncrowded or loaded topics hit (Puller 5.8x, Manstein-"fired" 4.8x, Sherman 4.6x).
- **Does a same-commander video split demand?** Mostly no, as long as ours is not a weaker copy: Up Next traffic (12.7 % on Thomas) comes FROM the big video on the same subject. It hurts when our title/banner looks like a clone of theirs (e.g. "LET THEM COME", "Turned Korea Around", "Most Decorated Marine"). Rule: same subject is fine, never the same angle.

## 4. Red flags the picker missed
- **The queue/backlog (the biggest one).** The sequencing advice is meaningless until the owner confirms which of Rokossovsky, Peng, Jackson, Meade, Sherman, Cleburne, Belisarius and Hannibal are public, scheduled, or unreleased. The scan shows only 7 public videos. Our Sherman also now competes with FH's Sherman (101k in 12 days); publish ours with a clearly different angle (ours is "Broke the Confederacy", which is fine).
- **The Puller date error** (13 d, not 22 d) under-scores him.
- **Licences:**
  - IWM Non-Commercial Licence = no monetised YouTube. Use Crown-copyright-expired copies from Commons.
  - Bundesarchiv CC-BY-SA = credit every photo in the description; share-alike applies to the image.
  - Soviet photos (Zhukov/Chuikov): PD-Russia does not always mean PD in the US (URAA); check each file.
  - Library of Congress / NARA / US Army / USMC photos are safe (US federal work).
  - AWI has paintings only (fine: Morgan worked without photos), but use PD paintings, not modern artworks (e.g. modern battle paintings on battlefields.org are copyrighted).
  - **vidIQ thumbnails of real people:** describe the face, never claim a likeness (already a rule).
- **Quote accuracy on banners:**
  - Patton "48 HOURS" is disputed.
  - Puller's "surrounded" line has several versions and is doubted.
  - Grant's "lick 'em tomorrow" rests on one anecdote.
  - Use quote marks only with a primary source in FACT_NOTES.
- **Sensitivity:**
  - Model and Guderian: Nazi-era generals. The tone must be clinical, with no "genius" worship in the thumbnail.
  - Sheridan's "Burning" of the Shenandoah, and his later Indian Wars quote ("the only good Indian…" is attributed to him; avoid it or address it).
  - Arnold: no glorifying treason (frame it as a tragedy).
  - Lettow-Vorbeck: colonial famine.
- **Map-friendliness:**
  - Puller's moves are battalion/regiment scale: plan close-up option-2 looks.
  - Eisenhower is a staff general (no tactical maps): the picker flagged him, and I agree to drop him.
  - Slim's jungle needs rivers to carry the maps.
  - Ridgway's Ripper is a broad front advance (hard to make dramatic): lead with Chipyong-ni.
- **Overlong stories:** Grant (4 years) and Patton (Sicily to the Bulge) need ruthless pruning to fit 15-17 min. Keep each move to one battle with one decision.
- **The overlap web:**
  - Grant: Chattanooga (Thomas, Cleburne) and Vicksburg (Sherman's queue video).
  - Patton: Cobra (Collins).
  - Puller: Chosin (Smith).
  - Model: Arnhem (Gavin) and Bagration (Rokossovsky).
  - Cross-links help end screens, but a viewer who sees the "same battle again" in the first minute may leave: never open on the overlapping battle.
