### 2.2 Hidden biases in files 1-3
1. **Survivorship.** The master table is built from each channel's TOP videos. The same subjects' flops elsewhere (e.g. Rommel on 4 channels, Ridgway at FH 1.5x) are listed but carry little weight in "demand". A subject's typical result is the median across channels, not its best cell. Patton: TG 10.9x / WG 3.8x (median ~7x). Ridgway: 15.5x / 1.5x. Zhukov: 8.5 / 2.4 / 0.85 / 0.6.
2. **One-off explosions.** TG Ridgway 1.36M and Ravelin Greene 1.12M are algorithm events, not subject proof. Our Greene remake (2,640 in 2 d) on Ravelin's mega-subject is direct evidence that the explosion does not transfer.
3. **Tiny-channel x-medians** (Cloaked 205x on a median of 377, WAT 100x on 747) are noise. Rank by v/d and raw views, not x.
4. **Cumulative views.** C&T rows are 146-513 days old and FH rows 1-22 days. Use v/d at a comparable age.
5. **No CTR or impressions for any competitor.** Every "the banner made it work" claim is inference. Agent 1 says so; the picker and critic then use banner rules as if proven. Treat the wording rules as tie-breakers.
6. **Our channel state at upload time.** Thomas (10-02) sat dead until its title fix. Smith (10-10) launched into a cold channel. 2 Para (10-17) and Morgan (10-21) launched while Thomas was hot. Gavin (10-26) and Greene (10-28) are both slow despite different eras. The variable may be **when** a video went up and what the channel's browse push was doing, not era. Only Studio data (impressions per video by day) can separate this.
7. **Our channel is being over-read.** 7 public videos, the oldest 28 days, 3 hits. "Fit" scores (0-20 in 2_list) and the critic's "seeded audience" theory rest on 3 data points. Use them as a gate, not a ranking input.

### 2.4 Patton first or Grant first, and the test that settles it
**Decision: produce Patton first, Grant second.** Both get made either way, so the real question is publish order, and the queue already holds 4 CW videos.

**The settling test (pre-registered, nearly free, ready before Patton publishes).** From Studio, take each video's **7-day impressions, CTR and views** for Collins and Gavin (WWII West), Rokossovsky (WWII East) and Peng (Korea), against Jackson and Meade (CW).
