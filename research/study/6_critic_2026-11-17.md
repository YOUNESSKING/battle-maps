## 2. Logic
**"Civil War is the only era working for us" is not supported.** Each era has 1 to 3 data points, and each is confounded by when the video went up:
- **Revolution:** Morgan launched while 2 Para was exploding.
- **WWII:** Gavin and Collins had the CV openings that CHANNEL_LOG blames for the loss. Patton launched cold, after two dead videos.
- **Korea:** Smith went out to 16 subscribers.
- **Civil War:** Thomas is the one clean data point for the era, because it launched to a cold channel and still caught fire after its retitle.

Grant's watch time per impression and Suggested CTR are not logged, and those are the numbers DATA_RULES actually uses. Studio's audience list also cuts against the study: "Korean War and Pacific Marine battles... commander profiles (**Puller, Ridgway**, Grant, Sherman)" (CHANNEL_LOG, 2026-10-31).

**Publish timing is the real gap in the study.** Sherman, Jackson and Meade are Civil War videos that are already made and still waiting. Sherman is Grant's own lieutenant, and FH's Sherman did 117K. It is the video that can ride Grant's sidebar *now*. A Sheridan made from scratch will be ready in 1-2 weeks, after Grant's launch window has closed. By then the queue already holds 3 Civil War videos, and the 4_meta rule allows at most 2 Civil War uploads in any 4. So making a 4th Civil War video next does not help DATA_RULES 1. It just creates a queue we cannot publish back to back.

**Fatigue.** I see no evidence of audience fatigue. The real risks are clustering and FH. FH has now covered Grant, Sherman, Thomas, Lee, Jackson, Chamberlain, Longstreet and Sheridan, so on any Civil War name we are the follower. "Proven era back to back" argues for publishing the Civil War videos we have already made, not for adding another one.

**Sheridan overlap with FH is worse than the study says.** FH's chapters (vidIQ description) are Move 1 Cedar Creek, Move 2 Five Forks, Move 3 Appomattox. The study proposes Third Winchester or Yellow Tavern, then Cedar Creek, then Five Forks to Appomattox, which is 2.5 of FH's 3 moves. The study's banner, **THE RIDE**, is FH's own angle ("He Rode Into a Rout"), and that contradicts the study's own advice to lead with Appomattox.

**Candidates the study missed or under-checked:**
- **J.E.B. Stuart:** 3,623 searches/month (vidIQ, today), more than Sheridan. Map-friendly ride around McClellan, Chancellorsville flank, Brandy Station. Same Confederate-hero handling as Jackson and Cleburne. He is a good *pairing* with Sheridan (Yellow Tavern).
- **Henry Knox:** Studio itself suggested him (CHANNEL_LOG 2026-11-02). The noble train of artillery and Dorchester Heights are a perfect animated-map story with no format competitor. The name search was blocked, so it is still unmeasured.
- **Anthony Wayne and George Rogers Clark:** both unmeasured because the searches were blocked. Hold them as Revolution reserves until Yorktown's and Marion's day 1-3 numbers say whether the Revolution is alive.
- **Forrest exclusion:** agree.
- **Chamberlain:** agree. FH did him on 12 Nov and CWD has 130K.
- **Winfield Scott:** a new era (Mexico 1847), no evidence for it. Skip.

## 3. Is Sheridan easy to show, and are the title and thumbnail strong and true?
**Easy to show: yes, if the moves differ from FH's:**
- Move 1: Yellow Tavern (May 1864, Stuart killed) or Third Winchester (19 Sep 1864, a combined cavalry charge). Use the `valley` map.
- Move 2: Cedar Creek, but told from the counterattack, not the ride.
- Move 3: Five Forks, then Sayler's Creek, then Appomattox Station (1-8 Apr 1865, getting ahead of Lee). New maps are needed west of Petersburg. Brady photos are public domain, and Read's painting *Sheridan's Ride* gives a recognisable context image.

**Tone risks:** the Valley "Burning" and his Indian Wars record. The "only good Indian" quote is disputed, so never use it as a fact.

**Title "| The Man Who Trapped Lee's Army": defensible, not exact.** Custer's division (Sheridan's command) took Appomattox Station on 8 April and blocked the road west, and Ord's and Griffin's infantry closed the trap on 9 April. Grant's own title already claims the Appomattox surrender. A better fit: "| The Man Who Cut Off Lee's Retreat", with a FACT_NOTES check.

**Thumbnail:**
- **THE RIDE:** reject. It copies FH, and it says nothing to a cold viewer.
- **CUT OFF:** better, over the Appomattox map.
