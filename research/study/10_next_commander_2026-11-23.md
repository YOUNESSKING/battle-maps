## Recommendation: #1 Andrew Jackson
- Famous: a president and a famous song, 20-25k monthly searches, and 3D clips are already pulling 90k+.
- Under-covered: no Top 3 video, and the best documentaries are 2018-2025.
- Fit: the 1812 era is adjacent to our proven Revolution and Civil War audience. The moves map cleanly (river bend, night road, canal line).
- Be honest in the script about British blunders (missing ladders) and Lafitte/Choctaw/free-men-of-colour roles; keep removal and slavery out of the three moves and out of the hook.
- Fallback #2: Washington "Brooklyn to Princeton", if the owner wants zero controversy.
