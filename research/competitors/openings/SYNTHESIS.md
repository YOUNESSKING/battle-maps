# Competitor openings vs ours: synthesis (2026-11-01)

Sources: the 9 vidIQ "video watch" analyses in this folder (one file per video, analysisText verbatim), our
`research/CHANNEL_LOG.md` (Studio numbers), `STYLE_LOCK.md` 1a + 1c, and each of our folders' `audio/timing.json`
(sentence start times). Competitor timestamps are vidIQ's; ours are from timing.json (narration start of the sentence).

**Limits, read first.**
- vidIQ gives no retention for other channels. Views are confounded by channel size, video age and topic, so "more views"
  is NOT proof that an opening works. Tactical Genius (TG) is far bigger than Warfare Genius (WG) and Footprints in History (FiH);
  compare within a channel before across channels.
- Where vidIQ's own analysis looks wrong or vague:
  - **Eisenhower (WG):** says the name is "spoken first at 1:16", but its own transcript has no name in the 1:16 narration; the name is
    on screen at 0:20 and 1:16 (card) and the first spoken "Dwight D. Eisenhower" in its transcript is at ~2:27. Table uses that.
  - **Patton (WG):** "first named 0:18" is an on-screen lower third only; the narration says "the general" throughout and the name is
    first spoken at ~2:43 in its transcript.
  - **Puller (FiH):** 0:03-0:06 and 0:06-0:09 carry the same narration line (duplicated); the seconds there are approximate.
  - **Guderian (WG):** says the surname is "visible on map counters at 0:02" - not checked; it conflicts with its own 0:57 "first named".
  - **"First number"** is a date in every competitor video (vidIQ counts "1950", "1943"...). The table gives the first COUNT (men, days...)
    separately where vidIQ names one.
  - **Shot length** is vidIQ's estimate (ranges like "6-10 s"), not a frame count.
- **Our Greene:** the owner listed `greene2/`, but CHANNEL_LOG's "named at 0:59" and STYLE_LOCK's "old Greene opening (the American
  Revolution was dying in the South)" match `greene/audio/timing.json`; `greene2/audio/timing.json` names Greene at 0:46. Which voice
  track the Oct 28 upload used is not certain from the repo; both rows are given.
- Our "picture changes" column is the lock target, not a measurement: no frame count was made for this file. Thomas, Morgan and
  2 Para were made before the 2026-10-09 hook lock.

## 2. What the top videos share (script and visuals)

Weighted toward the three biggest (TG Ridgway 1.36M, TG Patton 954k, WG Guderian 717k).

1. **Line 1 = a time + the stakes in one sentence; never a bare number.** "By the final weeks of 1950, the war in Korea had become a
   catastrophe" (Ridgway 0:00), "...saw amateurs" (TG Patton 0:00), "...preparing to lose the war" (TG Manstein 0:00), "...an order he
   knew would kill his own men" (Guderian 0:00). None of the 9 opens on a count. The three FiH videos and WG Patton/Eisenhower open on a
   date + a scene ("readers opened their morning paper"), which vidIQ rates as low stakes; they are also the lower-view ones.
2. **One crisis, then "one specific man" as its turn, spoken at 0:30-0:57.** TG Patton 0:22-0:30 "But there was one specific man...",
   TG Manstein 0:30 "one specific man was quietly drawing a very different map", Ridgway 0:31 "Washington sent one specific man",
   Guderian names at 0:57 after the frozen-order story. Same structure as our Thomas 0:47.
3. **A paradox/disgrace as the human detail, not a CV.** Patton "censured, relieved, sidelined" (0:37), Manstein "removed... exiled to
   an infantry corps" (0:46), Guderian "stripped of his command on Christmas Day... he was right" (0:27-0:38), Sherman "insane" (0:11),
   Puller "lost it ten days later" (0:10). All 9 have one; the biggest keep it to 5-15 s.
4. **The result is claimed as one line at 0:43-1:07, not at 10-15 s.** Patton 0:43 "German generals named him the most dangerous
   commander", Manstein 0:58 "Six weeks later the largest army in Europe had ceased to exist", Ridgway 0:55 "in roughly 100 days he
   reversed the course of the entire war". Guderian is the exception: his reversal ("he was right") lands at ~0:31.
5. **A fixed formula close:** "But what exactly set him apart...?" + "Let's take a closer look at X's three greatest tactical moves"
   (TG Ridgway 1:08-1:21, TG Patton 0:52-1:01, TG Manstein 1:03-1:12, WG Guderian 1:16, WG Eisenhower 2:23, WG Patton 2:39).
6. **TG: short intros, move 1 at 1:02-1:27; WG/FiH: 2:01-3:00.** Inside each channel the shorter intro has more views (TG Patton 1:02
   = 954k vs TG Manstein 1:12 = 251k shows it is not the only factor).
7. **Visuals are calmer than ours.** Ridgway: one map, units moving, 6-10 s per shot, a bio card, then archival film. TG Manstein 6-8 s.
   TG Patton has no map at all before move 1 (archival film, ~3.4 s). Music everywhere is a low drone; vidIQ notes "no overt battle sound
   effects" for TG Patton, only whooshes elsewhere. Name cards appear on screen before the name is spoken (Ridgway card 0:32 / spoken
   0:46; FiH Manstein 0:25 / 1:51).
8. **No three-battle preview in the TG hooks.** Only FiH Puller (1:48 montage) and the WG I/II/III title cards show the three moves.

## 3. Where we differ

- **Line 1:** our flops and Smith open on a count ("Fifteen hundred heavy bombers"); no competitor does. Our hits Morgan/Greene
  open like TG (time + stakes); Thomas opens like FiH (date + place) and still held ~70% at 0:30.
- **Name timing:** our hits name the hero late (Morgan 1:20, 2 Para 1:42); TG at 0:30-0:46. Our current rule (0:45-1:00) is at the
  late end of TG.
- **Promise timing:** our lock demands the result spoken by 10-15 s; the top three say it at ~0:31-1:07.
- **Pace and sound:** our lock wants a change every 2-4 s, a stamp/number card, a dive by 6 s and SFX on every beat; the 1.36M video
  changes picture every 6-10 s under a drone.
- **Preview:** we do name slam + 3 snap cuts; TG does not.
- **Background:** we add a separate background block after the title line (Collins 1:06-2:00, Cleburne 1:03-2:01, Smith 1:37
  "go back three months"); TG goes straight into move 1 and gives context inside it ("The first move was not a battle at all", Ridgway 1:22).
- **On-screen text:** our 1c rule (nothing before it is spoken) is stricter than every competitor.
- **CV:** both sides avoid birth/schooling in the TG videos. Where competitors do biography (FiH Puller, WG Eisenhower) it is ONE
  story with a throughline (the lost gold bar, the missed war), not a list - consistent with our finding that Gavin/Collins failed
  when they left the story for a list, not because biography appeared.

   promise). Do not change unless a test video beats 64% at 0:30.
