# Why @WarfareGeniuss gets consistent results (owner question 2026-11-06)
Data: research/competitors/WarfareGeniuss.md (full scan 2026-10-30, 9 long videos) + WarfareGeniuss_2026-11-06.md (rescan, 3 newest only); thumbnails research/competitors/thumbs/WarfareGeniuss_sheet.jpg.
- Floor and median: every video 10.7k+ views (median 35k, 5.4-5.7k subs); ours: most recent videos 0.5-3k, hits 39-123k.
- 1. Only household names: Guderian 717k, Eisenhower 299k, Patton 132k, Rommel 46k, Manstein 35k, Zhukov 23k, Bradley 25k, Montgomery 17k. Every one is a name viewers already search for and click. His one non-WWII video (Washington) is his lowest (10.7k). Our flops are lesser-known names (Gavin, Collins, Smith, Greene) or a name with a split reaction (Arnold).
- 2. One thumbnail formula, every time: realistic portrait on the right looking off-frame; a 3D battlefield diorama with blue vs red block armies, ONE big white arrow, fire + black smoke, small place tags and a small portrait pin; ONE emotional word in quotes on a red brush banner bottom-left ("PUNISHED", "HOPELESS", "A DECOY", "BETRAYED", "BURIED", "FORGOTTEN", "OVERRATED?", NO WAY OUT). Brand recognition in the feed.
- 3. One title formula: "<Name>'s Top 3 Genius/Legendary Tactical Moves | <injustice or paradox>" ("Fired for Being Right", "The General Who Never Saw Combat", "Why Stalin Exiled His Best General").
- 4. Length 19-21 min every time (ours 15-17): more minutes watched per click.
- 5. Steady cadence: ~1 video every 4 days, all in one era (WWII).
Takeaways for us: pick famous names (Grant first, then Sheridan or other household Civil War/WWII names even if a competitor covered them, labelled); keep one fixed thumbnail formula with ONE quoted emotional word; title with an injustice/paradox; aim ~18-20 min.
