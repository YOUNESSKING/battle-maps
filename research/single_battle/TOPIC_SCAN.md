# Single-battle story video: topic scan (vidIQ, 2026-11-08)

Goal: pick ONE battle for a new single-battle format ("X saw a chance... Y saw it coming", reference Redcoats & Rebels, R&R) that has demand and that our competitors have NOT covered in the last ~4 months (since ~2026-07-08). Proven eras first (DATA_RULES: American Revolution, Civil War).
vidIQ credits: 1,618 at start, 1,253 at end, **365 used** (22 channel pulls, 27 keyword lookups, 10 YouTube searches, 11 outlier searches). Nothing uploaded or posted.

## How to read the numbers (caveats)
- "Search/mo" = vidIQ `estimatedMonthlySearch` for the exact phrase (YouTube search). **"<750" means below vidIQ's reporting floor, NOT zero.** Phrases are single seeds ("battle of X"); "X explained / documentary" variants were not measured separately (the one try, "civil war battles explained", was <750).
- Video views are as of 2026-11-08 and depend on age; AI-labeled = vidIQ marks the video as AI-made. Small channels with huge views are listed as demand proof, not as a forecast for us.
- Channel coverage = `vidiq_channel_videos` long-form, most recent uploads per channel (it returns ~10-50 recent uploads, so for channels that upload daily the window reaches back only to the date shown). Scores are my estimates (0-5 each, max 20), not vidIQ numbers.
- "Our assets" = what exists in the repo (grep of `*/assets`). A basemap exists for: Gettysburg (meade/), Vicksburg + siege, Fort Donelson (grant/), Chancellorsville + Bull Run (jackson/), Saratoga + Valcour + Quebec (arnold/), Cowpens + Guilford (morgan/, greene/), Eutaw/Dan River (greene/), Chickamauga + Nashville (thomas/), Chattanooga + Bentonville + Ringgold (cleburne/), Atlanta (sherman/). **No basemap for Yorktown, Lexington & Concord, Bunker Hill, Trenton, Shiloh, Antietam, Fredericksburg** (only archive paintings/photos).
- "Ours already" = a video of ours already covers it (CHANNEL_LOG / folder names): Gettysburg (Meade #12), Vicksburg (Grant #16), Chancellorsville + Bull Run (Jackson), Saratoga (Arnold #17, published 2026-11-03, weak CTR 2.85%), Cowpens (Morgan "Top 3", Oct 21). A single-battle video would be a new format on the same ground, but it is fair to count it as a repeat.

## Top-3 recommendation
Rule applied: a battle with real search demand, no long-form story from any of the 22 channels in 4+ months, in a proven era, and an "enemy plan vs. hero saw it coming" shape.

1. **Yorktown, 1781** (Revolution; 17,580 searches/mo, highest of any proven-era battle; ABT map 117K and PBS 337K prove viewers; no long-form story from competitors).
   Angle: Cornwallis dug in at Yorktown because the Royal Navy was supposed to be his lifeline and escape route; Washington saw the Navy was the weak point, kept it secret from his own army, and marched ~450 miles to be there when de Grasse's fleet closed the Chesapeake (Battle of the Capes, 5 Sep). Enemy plan: a safe naval base. Hero saw it coming: the sea. Facts to verify in FACT_NOTES before use: march length, Capes date, Cornwallis storm escape attempt, surrender numbers (~7,000 per sources seen).
   Proposed title (A, locked format, estimate): "Britain Thought Yorktown Was a Safe Harbor. Washington Turned It Into a Trap" / alt "Cornwallis Trusted the Royal Navy. Washington Saw It Coming".
   Asset gap: needs a new Chesapeake/Yorktown basemap + the Trumbull painting for the end card; Greene's video already points here (Southern campaign leads to Yorktown), so it can run as the finale after Greene/Morgan.

2. **Lexington & Concord, 1775** (Revolution; 14,553/mo and +31% growth; weakest recent long-form competition of all, biggest video under 15K; Epic History's 821K is from April 2025, 19 months ago).
   Angle: General Gage's secret raid on Concord's powder store; the town knew hours before (spies, Revere/Dawes, Warren), moved the powder, and the militia pulled the British back along a 20-mile road. Enemy plan: a night raid nobody would see. Hero saw it coming: the colonial intelligence network, so the "surprise" arrived at an empty armoury. Facts to verify: size of the column (about 700), who warned whom, time of first shot, casualties, retreat distance.
   Proposed title (A, estimate): "Gage Sent 700 Redcoats to Seize Concord's Gunpowder. The Colonists Saw It Coming".
   Asset gap: small-area map (Boston - Lexington - Concord road), simpler than a campaign map.

3. **Saratoga, 1777** (Revolution; 8,795/mo; Ravelin proves demand with 363K + 137K + 86K in five months, all AI-labeled and one small channel; none of our 22 competitors cover it).
   Angle: Burgoyne's plan to cut New England off along the Hudson; Bemis Heights (Kosciuszko), Arnold's charge. Easiest to produce: arnold/saratoga HD maps exist. **Caveat:** it repeats our own Arnold #17 (published 3 Nov, Browse CTR 2.85%). Only choose this if the owner wants a fast build or a packaging test on the same ground. Runner-up with similar shape and more room to grow: **Bunker Hill** (6,902/mo, no map; Epic History 17 months old) or **Chancellorsville** (maps exist, "Hooker saw a chance, Lee saw it coming"; but search <750).
   Proposed title (A, estimate): "Burgoyne Saw a Chance to Split the Colonies at Saratoga. Gates' Engineer Saw It Coming" (check: attributing the Bemis Heights plan to Kosciuszko is a disputed claim; verify before use).

Not recommended now: **Gettysburg** (R&R just made 102K with the exact format on 23 Oct, plus Jeffrey 562K and Orders In Motion 2 Nov: the sidebar is full, and we already have Meade); **Antietam** (R&R's only 5K on it); WW2 wildcards (crowded, 0/3 for us).

## Tips for the format test (from the data, not claims)
- R&R's Gettysburg title format ("X Saw a Chance to Win the War at Y. Z Saw It Coming") got 102K; the same format on Antietam, D.C. 1814 and Little Bighorn got 5K / 0.8K / 11K. The format alone does not carry it: the topic's search demand decides (Gettysburg 8.5K/mo vs Antietam <750). That supports picking a battle with 10K+/mo.
- All 3 recommended battles are Revolution (a proven era) with no recent long-form from large channels; the sidebar next to them is small channels (Ravelin, ABT, Hillsdale), so a face-pop thumbnail is likely to win (DATA_RULES 3). This is an estimate, not a measurement.
- Check at build time: YouTube search for the exact title words and note what sits at the top (the 245th Yorktown anniversary was 19 Oct, so ABT's map could linger in suggested).
