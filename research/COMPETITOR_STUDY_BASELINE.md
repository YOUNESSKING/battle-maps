## 5. Planned method
1. Per competitor channel: pull every long video (title, views, length, date), compute outlier x median, tag by war/era/side/format, and note thumbnail text/type and title pattern.
2. Pool every commander across all channels; score each: demand (cross-channel views), competitor saturation, our-channel fit (WW2 Allied/Korea/comeback pattern), story visualizability (3 map-friendly battles, licensed archive, no stock-footage dependence), source quality (English accounts, Official Records), sensitivity.
3. Remove everyone in section 1. Output: ranked list of 15-20 with title/thumbnail text per top 10, risk notes, and a recommended next 5.
4. Judge it with independent agents (as with the map-detail test) before the owner sees the final list.
