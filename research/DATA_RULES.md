# DATA RULES (LOCKED, owner 2026-11-02: "Let's make these rules")
Source: research/CHANNEL_LOG.md, "Traffic sources, days 1-3" (Morgan, 2 Para, Greene, Gavin, Smith, Patton). YouTube judges a new video in
TWO STAGES: (1) it gives a day-1/2 test batch, sized by how the channel is doing right then; (2) the batch decides: watch time earned per
impression (CTR x average view duration) ~18 s+ and Suggested CTR ~3.5%+ -> expansion (Morgan, 2 Para); ~13-15 s -> flat (Greene, Patton);
~8 s -> cut (Gavin). Retention at 0:30/0:40 alone did NOT decide it (2 Para 37% at 0:40 = hit; Greene 50% = flat). "Crowded topic" did
NOT explain it (research/saturation/SATURATION.md). These rules replace the 2026-10-31 "0:30 >= 64% or cut" data rule.

1. **Publish order = momentum.** Upload the strongest videos back to back (2-4 days apart) so each launches on the previous one's audience.
   Never follow a flop with an important video. Lead with our proven eras (American Revolution, Civil War); WWII/Korea only after a hot
   upload (WWII so far 0/3). Order of the waiting queue is decided with these rules before uploads resume.
2. **Minutes watched is the target.** Aim for ~18 s of watch time per Browse impression: e.g. 4.5% CTR x ~6:40 average view duration, or
   5% x ~6:00. Scripts must hold viewers through move 1 and into move 2 (Morgan averaged 6:17), not only past 0:40. Keep the opening rule
   (STYLE_LOCK 1a): it gets them in; the moves must keep them (each move opens on its own crisis, ends on a result card + a question that
   pulls into the next move).
3. **Win the click in the SIDEBAR too.** Before publishing, search the topic on YouTube and look at the top videos our thumbnail will sit
   next to; make ours visibly different (our animated-map manoeuvre + face, 1-3 true words). Target Suggested CTR ~3.5%+.
4. **Measure every upload the same way** with the standard Studio prompt below (days 1-3, by traffic source) and log it in CHANNEL_LOG.
   If day-3 Suggested CTR < ~2.5% or watch time per impression < ~15 s: Test & Compare a new thumbnail/title first.
5. **An untested video is not dead.** If a video got almost no day-1 impressions (Smith ~160, Patton ~1,300), give it a fresh thumbnail
   (and title test) once the channel is hot again.

## The standard Studio prompt (one video at a time)
For "<exact title>", first 3 days after publishing, day by day:
Browse features: impressions, views, click-through rate.
Suggested videos: impressions, views, click-through rate.
Retention at 0:30 and 0:40. Average view duration.
Numbers only, in a table.
