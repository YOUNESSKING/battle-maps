## Film (archive.org)
### Battle of Moscow, winter 1941
- **MoscowStrikesBack**, "Moscow Strikes Back" (Artkino/Soviet Central Newsreel Studio, 1942, 55:34, Oscar 1943). Marked Public Domain Mark 1.0 on archive.org. PD basis: Soviet 1942 film, US release, listed PD (PD-Russia-1996 logic). Best single source for T-34s/ski troops in snow, counterattack Dec 1941. Time ranges not yet scouted.
- **82094DefenseOfMoscowVwr**, "Defenders of Moscow 1941" (Soviet film for US audience, 16:38, Periscope Film upload; no licence tag, source film is 1941-42 Soviet footage). Useful: armoured train 3:50-5:03; cavalry guards 5:56-6:32 and charge 7:11-7:52; howitzers in snow 6:33-7:10; Tu-2/bombers 9:38-12:12; **Rokossovsky on horseback planning 12:16-12:33**; tank assault 12:45-14:13; German POWs shivering 14:54-16:05.
- **the-battle-of-russia-1943** (also BattleOfRussiaI/II, 5-the-battle-of-russia), Capra "Why We Fight: The Battle of Russia" (US Army, 1943, ~83 min). PD: US government work (PD Mark / publicdomain). Moscow winter, Kursk/Stalingrad sections; contains some Soviet footage and animated maps (maps off-style, skip). Time ranges not scouted.
- 59304SovietFilmGermansInMoscow (1970s Soviet compilation, ~5:39): licence unclear, avoid unless footage traced to 1941-42 originals.

### Kursk 1943
- **76694-russian-film-battle-of-kursk** (Soviet 1945 documentary, Periscope upload, 17:30; no licence tag, 1945 Soviet film, PD-Russia-1996 logic but flag for owner). Ranges: Soviet T-34 column 1:47-2:24; night artillery 3:05; Katyusha launch 4:01; burning Elefant/T-34s 4:18-4:36; Il-2 take-off 6:00; counteroffensive 9:45; **Rokossovsky 11:07**; urban fighting/bunkers 13:25-13:39; surrendering Germans 13:39; Oryol liberated 14:59; aftermath 16:23.
- gov.archives.arc.38964, "Soviet Army hurls back Nazis on 2,000 mile front" (United News, 1943, 10:13). **PD: US National Archives, CC0.** Russian troops advancing behind artillery and tanks (part 1 only, Part 1 starts at 0:00).
- Not usable: German Wochenschau uploads (Nr 672-675, YouTube mirrors; German newsreels, status unclear), "Soviet Storm" (CC BY-NC-ND), Periscope "Liberation of Kiev" (BY-NC-ND tag).

### Parade of the Vanquished, Moscow, 17 July 1944
- **52874ArmyScreenMagazineMassachuesttsShoreLeave**, Army-Navy Screen Magazine #47 (US Army Signal Corps, 1944; 17:41). PD: US government work. Segment "Moscow Parade" **9:28-13:17**: 57,000 German POWs marched through Moscow, silent crowds, rail yard 10:40, water trucks washing the street 12:42. Skip the rest of the reel. **Best candidate for this beat.**
- Twitter MFA Russia clip (archive.org twitter-1284024478998159360): modern post of original Soviet footage; licence unclear, avoid.

### Victory Parade, Moscow, 24 June 1945
- **Parad_colort_Pobeda**, "Victory parade in colour" (1945 Soviet colour film, 18:41, no licence tag; 1945 Soviet film, PD-Russia-1996 logic; flag for owner). Rokossovsky commanded the parade (on a grey horse), Zhukov reviewed on a white one; scout 0:00-3:00 for the commanders.
- vts-03-1_20230310 "Parad Pobedy 1945" (DVD rip, files tiny/broken). Skip.
- gov.archives.arc.39066, "Gigantic parade marks May Day in Moscow" (United News, 1945): 1 May 1945 parade, NOT the Victory Parade; **CC0 / US Archives**. Only for generic Red Square.
- gov.archives.arc.39171, "Victory Round-Up" (United News 1945, CC0): Zhukov receiving Keitel's surrender at Berlin; Zhukov image only.
