## Background: Vyazma-Bryansk, October 1941
- "More than half a million Soviet soldiers cut off" (script) / OVER HALF A MILLION ENCIRCLED (map): the Vyazma and Bryansk pockets cost the Red Army roughly 1 million men in total; the Germans claimed ~673,000 prisoners (commonly cited 600-670k captured). "Over half a million encircled" is conservative and safe. (Wikipedia, Battle of Moscow / Vyazma-Bryansk; double-check against Glantz.)
- Overview maps: Finland shown Axis from 22 June 1941 (it entered the war 25-26 June); Romania shown Soviet-side on the Aug 1944 map (changed sides 23 Aug). Fronts per date: FACT_NOTES_east.md.

## Move 1: Volokolamsk highway, Oct-Dec 1941
- 16th Army (2nd formation) formed 10 Aug 1941 near Yartsevo under Rokossovsky; after the Vyazma encirclement (Oct 1941) its HQ was sent to the Volokolamsk axis with scratch forces: 316th Rifle Division (Panfilov), Dovator's cavalry, Moscow military-school cadets, later Katukov's 4th Tank Brigade (= 1st Guards Tank Brigade from 11 Nov; its Mtsensk ambushes were early Oct, before it joined 16th Army).
- Gemini "Oct-Nov": CORRECTED to mid-October to December. Volokolamsk fell 27 Oct; the German second-phase offensive started 15-16 Nov.
- Istra reservoir: CORRECTED. Not a "secret end-run". Rokossovsky asked Zhukov to pull back behind the Istra reservoir (~18 Nov); Zhukov refused; he appealed to Shaposhnikov (Chief of the General Staff), who approved; Zhukov revoked it. Source: Rokossovsky's memoir (Zhukov's account differs). Script presents it as Rokossovsky's account.
- Germans crossed the frozen reservoir ~25 Nov; 16th Army fell back line by line (Istra - Kryukovo - Krasnaya Polyana) without being encircled. A German reconnaissance unit reached Khimki on ~2 Dec, about 30 km from the Kremlin.
- Temperatures: "-30 °F" WRONG; lowest recorded in the period about -28.8 °C. Script: "almost thirty degrees below zero" (Celsius, not stated as F).
- "Siberian reserves counterattacked with Rokossovsky on 5 Dec": GARBLED. Counteroffensive began 5-6 Dec on the Kalinin/Western Fronts; 16th Army joined; Kryukovo retaken ~8 Dec, Istra ~11 Dec (from memory: double-check). Script: "within days 16th Army retook Kryukovo, then Istra".
- Hoepner: dismissed 8 Jan 1942 for ordering a withdrawal without Hitler's permission (not for the November halt). Script: as corrected.
- Force numbers (16th Army 60-80k; Germans 100k+/400 tanks): UNSUPPORTED. Script gives no exact strengths for move 1; "outnumbered, outgunned" only.
- Panfilov's 28: myth confirmed (1948 Soviet prosecutor's probe: story invented by Krasnaya Zvezda journalists; several of the "28" survived). The fight at Dubosekovo on 16 Nov did happen; Gen. Panfilov was killed 18 Nov. Script mentions the myth in one line.
- No reliable 16th-Army-level casualty figure: move 1 ends with a RESULT card (closest German approach ~30 km; Hoepner dismissed), not a casualty card. **Owner decision flagged.**

## Move 2: northern face of Kursk, 5-12 July 1943
- Forces: OK. Central Front 711,575 men (510,983 combat), ~1,785 tanks; 9th Army ~335,000 (223,000 combat). Script rounds: "over seven hundred thousand" vs "about three hundred and thirty-five thousand".
- "Six belts, 200 miles deep": WRONG. The whole Kursk system (both fronts + reserves) had ~6 belts, 130-150 km+ deep. Central Front's own sector: three main defensive belts plus front and army lines. Script: "three belts in his sector, behind them more lines".
- Concentration: 13th Army (Pukhov) held a sector ~40 km wide on the Orel-Kursk railway axis (Ponyri / Olkhovatka), reinforced with the 4th Artillery Breakthrough Corps. Script: "a sector about forty kilometres wide". Percentages not used (unverified).
- Counter-preparation: CORRECTED. Ordered ~02:00-02:20 on 5 July by Rokossovsky with Zhukov's agreement after captured German sappers gave the attack time; about 2-3 hours BEFORE the German bombardment (05:00 / attack ~05:30), not "minutes". Effect limited (fired at estimated positions) but it delayed and disrupted the attack. Script says this honestly.
- Ponyri fighting 6-12 July, the station changing hands; Ferdinands (sPzJgAbt 653/654) and Tigers (sPzAbt 505). "Stalingrad of the Kursk salient" = Paul Carell's phrase: script says "German veterans later called it a little Stalingrad" -> changed to "has been called" (attribution-safe).
- Advance: 9th Army got ~10-15 km (Gemini 10-12 OK-ish). Script: "barely ten to fifteen kilometres".
- Model halted 11-12 July as Operation Kutuzov struck the Orel bulge behind him (12 July).
- Casualties: CORRECTED. Central Front 33,897 total (15,336 killed/missing + 18,561 wounded/sick; Krivosheev). Gemini's "33,859 killed + 59,000 wounded" wrong. 9th Army ~20,000-23,000 for 5-11/12 July (Frieser / Zetterling range; double-check).

## Move 3: Bagration / Bobruisk, June 1944
- "Two main blows, Comrade Stalin": the exact words are NOT documented. The story is from Rokossovsky's memoir: he insisted on two breakthroughs (Rogachev in the north, Parichi in the south); Stalin twice sent him to the next room to "think it over", then accepted: "Your confidence speaks for your sound judgement" (memoir translation, quoted in Wikipedia). Zhukov disputed the story. Script: "According to Rokossovsky's own memoirs ..." + one line that Zhukov remembered it differently.
- Dates: CORRECTED. Bagration opened 22-23 June; Rokossovsky's 1st Belorussian Front attacked 24 June with both prongs (north: 3rd + 48th Armies from Rogachev; south: 65th + 28th Armies, 1st Guards Tank Corps and Pliev's cavalry-mechanised group through the marshes). Ring closed 27 June; Bobruisk fell 29 June; Rokossovsky promoted Marshal of the Soviet Union 29 June 1944 (date from memory, double-check).
- "Entire tank armies erupt from the swamps": WRONG. It was 65th Army (Batov) with 1st Guards Tank Corps and Pliev's group. The southern prong broke through first; the northern prong made slow progress on day one (from memory/standard accounts, double-check).
- Corduroy roads, swamp "wet-shoes" (mokrostupy) and rafts: in Rokossovsky's memoir and standard accounts; script keeps it short.
- German belief: OKH expected the main summer blow south of the Pripyat (Ukraine) and moved armour to Army Group North Ukraine. OK.
- Pocket: CORRECTED. Bobruisk pocket ~50,000 killed, ~20,000 captured, ~12,000 escaped (Wikipedia, Bobruysk offensive). Gemini "70,000 trapped" = upper bound. Script: "tens of thousands killed or captured"; casualty card ~50,000 killed / ~20,000 captured.
- Busch: relieved 28 June (with 9th Army's Jordan), replaced by Model. OK.
- Totals: ~400,000 German losses (Frieser 399,102) are for the WHOLE operation (four fronts), not Rokossovsky alone. Script states it as Army Group Centre's total.
- Soviet losses "65k killed / 150k wounded": UNSUPPORTED. Whole operation ~178,500 irrecoverable + ~587,000 sanitary (Krivosheev; double-check). Not used per front.
- Parade of the Vanquished, 17 July 1944: 57,000 prisoners marched through Moscow (OK).
