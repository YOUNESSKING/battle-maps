## Other on-screen facts (all from FACT_NOTES.md unless noted)
- 16th Army: 316th Rifle Div (Panfilov), Dovator cavalry group, Moscow military-school cadets, Katukov's 4th Tank Bde = 1st Guards Tank Bde from 11 Nov.
- German side: 4th Panzer Group (Hoepner). Corps labels on the counters (V Army Corps, XL and XLVI Panzer Corps) follow the
  brief; their exact sectors per date are NOT verified, so the map shows them as generic "4 PZ GROUP" sub-counters with corps names only.
- Istra reservoir episode: Rokossovsky's own memoir (Zhukov's account differs): asked ~18 Nov, Zhukov REFUSED, Shaposhnikov APPROVED, Zhukov CANCELLED.
- Germans crossed the frozen reservoir ~25 Nov. Closest approach ~30 km (Khimki recon, 2 Dec). Coldest ~ -28.8 C -> card "-28 °C".
- Panfilov killed 18 Nov; "28 Panfilov men" = Soviet propaganda (1948 prosecutor's probe), the Dubosekovo fight was real.
- Hoepner dismissed 8 Jan 1942 for ordering a withdrawal without permission.
- Not verified / approximate: exact village coordinates of Dubosekovo (~56.00 N 36.06 E) and Krasnaya Polyana (~56.01 N 37.44 E); the
  front lines outside the 16th Army sector; the mid-October line north of Volokolamsk.
