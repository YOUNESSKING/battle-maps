# Channel branding

## Name
**The War Table** (recommended). Alternatives: Commander's Map · Battle Genius · The Map Room · Great Commanders.
Check the handle is free on YouTube before committing (@TheWarTable, or @WarTableHistory as a fallback).

## Description (YouTube "About")
The War Table breaks down history's greatest commanders through their three greatest tactical moves, told on animated battle maps.

From Hannibal at Cannae to Nathanael Greene in the Carolinas, every video shows you the situation, what the enemy believed, what the general saw that no one else did, and exactly how the battle unfolded, unit by unit, on the map.

New commander every week. Tell us in the comments who we should cover next.

Sources are listed in every video description. Music by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), CC BY 4.0, and public-domain recordings.
