## Best vivid details that ARE verified
1. Dead River 21-22 Oct: rain, S.S.W. gale, trees falling; river rose 8-10 ft overnight, bateaux "under water almost out of sight"; "From a Dead river it had now become live enough" (Senter).
2. Dearborn's Newfoundland dog eaten 1 Nov by Goodrich's men, bones pounded for broth (Dearborn, Henry).
3. Senter: shaving soap, pomatum, lip salve, shoe leather, cartridge boxes eaten.
4. Planned 180 miles, became ~350; 1,100 became ~600 (Codman).
5. Night crossing past HMS Lizard and Hunter, ebb tide, muffled paddles (Codman).
6. Paper in every cap: "Liberty or death!", last day of enlistments, blizzard (Codman).
7. Arnold in the hospital, pistols loaded, sword on bed (Senter).
8. Morgan, last to surrender, gave his sword to a priest (WQ).

- "Hurricane" as a meteorological fact (use "storm"); "8 ft" without "8 to 10".
- "Enos took 300" (three companies, ~450 with invalids); "Enos was a deserter" (he was acquitted).
- "Left leg" at Quebec until a source is found.
- "Garrison 1,800 breakdown" exact splits; "100 guns".
- "Arnold's men were called the American Hannibal's army" at the time; "65 tons"; "400 lb bateaux" until sourced.
- "White" slips; the sprig of hemlock came first (night sign).
- Arnold took/nearly took Quebec; Montgomery captured anything.

## Numbers per side (ranges)
American: start ~1,100 (950 on 12 Oct); Enos back ~450 (3 companies + ~150 invalids) late Oct; arrived ~600 (500 fit); Montgomery ~300 + Canadians; assault ~900 in 4 columns; ~400 lost. British: 1,178-1,248 on the roster, up to 1,800 later; 2 warships (Lizard 28 guns, Hunter); loss 5 killed, 14 wounded.
Distance 350 mi (plan 180); Great Carrying Place ~12 mi, ~1,000 ft climb (WA); time Fort Western 25 Sep to 9 Nov (45 d).
