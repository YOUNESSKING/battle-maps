## Best vivid details (verified at WA/WQ level)
1. Dearborn's dog eaten, bones pounded for broth (WA).
2. Dead River rose to the camp at dawn 22 Oct (WA).
3. Planned 180 miles in 20 days; became 350 miles; 1,100 became ~600 (WA).
4. 30-ton figure none; the brass six-pounder on a sled abandoned in a drift (WQ).
5. Morgan, last to surrender, handed his sword to a priest (WQ).
6. Blizzard attack at 4-5 am by flares; Montgomery dead in seconds (WQ).

## Crisis moment to open Move 1 (one-two sentences, safe)
"Early on 22 October the Dead River rose in the night until it reached the men's camp: boats were wrecked, food was ruined, and 1,100 men were still 100+ miles from Quebec." (Distance remaining: UNVERIFIED, drop the figure if unchecked.) Alternative: Dearborn's dog going into the pot.
Hook candidate: stakes = Canada as a 14th colony / the British northern door (Quebec) with winter closing; hard number = 1,100 men left, about 600 arrived; disaster = flood and Enos turning back; payoff = 31 Dec Quebec in a blizzard. Keep Arnold's treason out of the opening beyond the one-line rule.

## Do not say
- "Arnold took Quebec" or "nearly took Quebec" without hedge; the assault failed.
- "Left leg" without a source in hand; "Carleton disguised"; "cartridge-box soup" (unverified); exact bateaux survivor count.
- "Montgomery captured the city": he died with the attack.
