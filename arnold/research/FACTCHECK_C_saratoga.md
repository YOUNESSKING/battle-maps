## Controversies / treason
- 19 Feb 1777 Congress promoted five juniors (Gemini): UNVERIFIED here. WP: passed over, resignation letter 11 Jul 1777 refused by Washington.
- Court-martial: convicted on two minor charges; Washington's reprimand in general orders 6 Apr 1780 ("Imprudent and improper" re the wagon matter) - CONFIRMED date and phrase via Founders search summary (page not opened). Verdict date 26 Jan 1780 (WP only).
- Peggy Shippen married 8 Apr 1779 (WP). André captured Sat 23 Sep 1780 near Tarrytown (WP). Offer £20,000; paid £6,315 plus £360 pension (WP). Arnold's letter from HMS Vulture to Washington dated 25 Sep 1780 (WP cites it) so flight = **25 Sep** (WP prose says 24 Sep; its own letter date contradicts). André hanged 2 Oct 1780. Vulture driven off 22 Sep by militia fire (WP).
- **ONE treason line:** "Three years later, Benedict Arnold tried to sell West Point to the British for 20,000 pounds, and the hero of Saratoga became the name of a traitor." Alt (NPS): the Boot Monument "does not mention Arnold by name".

## Best verified vivid details
Gates' 12 Oct report praising "Gallant General Arnold" who "forced the Enemy's Breast Work"; Arnold's "Late in the Day ... some Fun ... Before Sun Set" (NPS); Wilkinson's wounded-horse detail; Breymann's camp open at the rear (NPS: Germans "retreated to their tents in the rear" and fled into darkness; Arnold demanded surrender); Fraser fell leading the rearguard; Burgoyne's camp abandoned that night; Boot monument has no name.
