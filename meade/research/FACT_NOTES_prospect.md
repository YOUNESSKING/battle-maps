## Lines (13 Dec, approximate)
- Confederate front: along / just west of the RF&P from Hamilton's Crossing (Archer on Prospect Hill) north through the ~600-yard gap
  (Lane's OR report; between Archer and Lane) and Lane, Pender, Deep Run, then NW to Lee's Hill and Marye's Heights (stone wall).
- Gregg's brigade on the Military Road 500-600 yards behind the gap (Lane, FACT_NOTES).
- Union: Franklin on the plain between the river and the Stage Road (Deep Run - Pelham's Corner); Sumner in the town.
- Salient after the break-in (move1-5): from the gap to the Military Road ("over half a mile", Meade's report), then closed again.
