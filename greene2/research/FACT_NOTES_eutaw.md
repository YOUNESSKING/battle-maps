# FACT NOTES: Eutaw Springs battlefield map (`eutaw`, BATTLEFIELD agent)

Scenes: move3b (eutaw-3..7), move3c (eutaw-8). Option 2 (glowing fronts, no fill). Geography drawn by hand (very flat coastal plain):
`tools/make_eutaw_geo.py` -> `assets/media/eutaw_1781_ref.jpg` + `eutaw_geo.json`.

## Geography (lat, lon) and sources
Sources: American Battlefield Trust Eutaw Springs map, SC historic-site marker / Nominatim "Eutaw Springs Battle Ground"
(33.4074 N 80.2986 W, on Old Number Six Hwy = the house site), Lee's *Memoirs* + Greene's report (memory).
- **River road** (Congaree / Charleston road) from the west (Burdell's plantation, Greene's approach) east toward Charleston; drawn on the
  line of modern SC 6 / Old Number Six Hwy.
- **Eutaw house** (brick, two storeys) drawn at 33.4074 N 80.2975 W, north of the road; **walled garden** east of the house
  (high brick wall, drawn ~60 x 50 m, exaggerated).
- **British camp clearing** west of the house, tents drawn by the scene (both sides of the road).
- **Springs** 33.4060 N 80.2928 W; **Eutaw Creek** runs north then north-west to the Santee (approximate course).
- **Blackjack thicket** along the creek on the British right (north of the camp clearing) - Marjoribanks.
- **Santee River** along the top (today Lake Marion; course approximate). No modern places shown.
- **Exaggeration:** ~1.4x (American lines ~600-650 m west of the house).

## Units shown
- British line (N -> S) in front of the camp: 3rd (Buffs), Cruger's loyalists, New York Volunteers, 63rd, 64th; Marjoribanks's flank
  battalion in the thicket (right); Coffin's cavalry in reserve (left). New York Volunteers (Maj. Sheridan) in the brick house
  (FACT_NOTES.md correction row: the house = Sheridan, the thicket = Marjoribanks).
- Americans: 1st line militia (Henderson's SC state troops north, N. Carolina militia, Marion, Pickens, Lee's Legion south);
  2nd line Continentals (N. Carolina, Virginia, Maryland); reserve Washington's horse + Kirkwood's Delawares. Two American light guns
  fire at the house in eutaw-8.
- Badges: Greene (photo), Otho Williams (photo, eutaw-6), Stewart / Sheridan / Marjoribanks = initials (no licensed portrait).

## Cards / numbers (exactly as FACT_NOTES.md)
- Counters: AMERICANS ~2,200, BRITISH ~2,000; "CHARLESTON ~50 MILES" (script "some fifty miles").
- Rooting party digging sweet potatoes captured (Gemini, standard accounts).
- ROUNDS FIRED ticker to 17 "BY GREENE'S OWN ACCOUNT" (memory).
- Casualty card: AMERICAN 139 killed, 375 wounded, 8 missing (~522) | BRITISH 85 killed, 351 wounded, 257 missing (~693 = more than a
  third of the army).
- 9 September: Stewart falls back toward Charleston (Monck's Corner).
