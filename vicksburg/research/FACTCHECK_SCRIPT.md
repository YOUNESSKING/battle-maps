## Counts
Rows checked: 102 (some rows pack several claims). OK 76, HEDGE 14, FIX 12 (rows 5, 15, 38, 44, 47, 48, 56, 66, 69, 79, 92, 102; rows 47 and 48 are one passage, so 11 distinct items below).

## FIX items (summary)
1. S01 "seven weeks later" is nine weeks after the crossing (30 Apr to 4 Jul = 65 days).
2. S02 Grant "forty-one" during the winter scene (born 27 Apr 1822; he turned 41 on 27 April).
3. S06 Banks: Grant never expected Banks to "come up from New Orleans"; Banks (on the Red River) could not reach Port Hudson before 10 May, i.e. about a week, not "weeks".
4. S06 "a second army gathering under Johnston" is early: Johnston reached Jackson on the night of 13 May.
5. S07 "McPherson's entire corps" and "heat" (not sourced; only smoke and dust are documented).
6. S08 "the most important thing taken at Jackson was a piece of paper": the courier gave it to McPherson on the road; it was not captured in Jackson.
7. S09 "two more divisions under McClernand": McClernand had four divisions on the field.
8. S09 "one bridge led west over Baker's Creek" and "Three roads crossed it": the Confederates had more than one crossing; roads met at/led to the hill.
9. S09 Confederate losses "by Grant's count over three thousand killed and wounded and about three thousand captured": Grant's figure is about twice the modern one (about 3,800 total).
10. S11 "He had crossed the river twenty days earlier": 22 days before the 22 May assault; Grant's "twenty days" is as of the 19th.
11. S12 "an idea that everyone around him thought was madness": overstated.

## Things a knowledgeable viewer would call wrong or misleading in the comments
1. **"Nobody was waiting for them" / "no supply line"**: fine, but expect "Blair's 200 wagons" and the Yazoo supply road opened 19-21 May; the script is safe because it says "no supply line" only for the march to Big Black.
2. **Grant's casualty claims for Champion Hill**: the Confederate count is the one most likely to be corrected (Grant's "3,000 captured" vs about 2,400 missing). Row 79 fixes it.
3. **McClernand**: Grant's blame is one side; later historians note terrain and Loring's presence on the Raymond road, and that Carr and Osterhaus did fight. Rows 69 attribute the claim to Grant.
4. **"Hill changed hands three times"** is avoided (good); keep it as Hovey's phrase only.
5. **The Big Black "line was taken in minutes"**: the 3-minute figure is a single-source (Bearss) claim; the script says "minutes", which is safe.
6. **Sherman's greatest-campaign line**: do not put it in quote marks on screen (Grant disclaims the wording).
7. **Sherman's 8 April letter** is not a protest against the Bruinsburg crossing (it predates the orders); the script's S02 placement right after the plan could read that way. Add "on the eighth of April" if the card shows the date. Sherman's real doubt about the march was the May "road will be jammed" note, which S06 uses correctly.
8. **Vicksburg "closed to the North"**: Union gunboats and transports passed from April; viewers may mention Farragut (1862) and the river being passable in part. Safe as written because it says "while they held".
9. **Port Hudson "whole river in Union hands"**: guerrilla fire and Confederate batteries on the banks persisted; Grant and Lincoln used the same phrase. Acceptable.
10. **Pemberton "Let the river and the swamps do the work"** is not his documented plan; row 11 softens it.
11. **Raymond heat**: not in any source read; remove (row 48).
12. **Plaque dates**: 15 MAY for the council of war (held 14 May).
13. **Grant's age**: 40 until 27 April 1863 (row 15).
14. **Surrender number**: nearly thirty thousand / 29,500 is NPS-based; Grant's own text says 31,600 and lists 172 cannon. A commenter quoting 31,600 is quoting Grant; the script's hedge is fine.
