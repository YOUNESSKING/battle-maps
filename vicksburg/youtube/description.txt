In the spring of 1863, one city stood between the Union and the Mississippi River: Vicksburg. In just eighteen days, one Union army fought five battles to reach it, with no supply line behind it, marching between two Confederate armies.

This is the Vicksburg campaign told on animated 3D maps: Porter's gunboats running the guns at night, the landing at Bruinsburg, Port Gibson, Grant's decision to cut loose from his base, Raymond, Jackson and the spy's message, Champion Hill, the burning bridge at the Big Black, the assaults of May, the siege and the surrender of nearly thirty thousand men on the Fourth of July.

Which moment decided it: the night run past the guns, the turn toward Jackson, or Champion Hill? Tell us in the comments, and tell us which campaign we should cover next.
