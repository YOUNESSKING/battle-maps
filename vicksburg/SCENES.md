# Vicksburg 1863: scene plan (creative direction for the scene authors)

Every scene = `vicksburg/sequences/SNN/seq.js` built with the engine (`vicksburg/engine/README.md`), times local to the scene
(scene t = 0 at the section's first audio sample minus the lead-in; `audio/words.json` gives every word time; `tools/scene_times.py SNN` prints them).
Look = v8 clip exactly (`research/style_tests/gm3d/clip/contact_sheet.jpg`): tilted 3D terrain, haze, cumulus with ground shadows,
Style W boxes + soft arrows in-scene, generals' banners on poles, GM overlays (date plaque/clock top-left, THE ARMIES legend bottom-left,
wax-seal timeline bottom, medallion cards, labels, ledger top-right, oval painting insets, quote cards, 3D gold title).

## Global rules (all scenes)
1. Nothing appears before its word (cards, labels, numbers, stamps, banners of a named general). Re-check against words.json.
2. Every visible gun / volley / fire is a cue (sound) and every cue is visible. No unseen barrages.
3. Something new every 2-4 s; the camera always drifts (push, pan, slow yaw); hard cuts only on a section change or inside a cloud dive; day/night by a 0.6-1 s dissolve.
4. Union = Style W blue boxes (#2c57b7 family), Confederates = red (#be3a2a); captured units turn grey in place; retreats = road columns that leave frame (never vanish).
5. Banners: Grant (blue, US 34 stars), Pemberton (red, Stars and Bars); other generals only as medallion cards (Sherman, McPherson, McClernand, Porter, Bowen, Gregg, Johnston, Loring, Hovey, Logan, Lawler) unless a scene needs one banner per side.
6. Date plaque: rolls on each spoken date; DAY N counter (DAY 1 = 30 Apr) on the plaque's sub-line from S04 on.
7. One big card at a time; the legend and timeline step out when a big card/inset is up.
8. Ending of each scene: 0.8 s hold, then the next scene starts (dissolve handled at assembly: 12 frames crossfade between scenes).
9. Labels in title case serif (Cormorant), carved river names in italics on their word.
