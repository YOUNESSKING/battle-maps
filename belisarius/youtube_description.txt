How did one general keep beating armies twice his size? Belisarius, the "Last of the Romans", defeated the Persians at Dara, destroyed the Vandal kingdom at Tricamarum, and held Rome with about 5,000 men against the entire Gothic nation. These are his three greatest tactical moves, told with animated battle maps.
