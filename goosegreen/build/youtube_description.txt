Goose Green: 2 Para's Top 3 Legendary Tactical Moves | The Battle Britain Wasn't Supposed To Win

May 1982. Five hundred British paratroopers, a thousand Argentine defenders dug in across an open isthmus, and a BBC broadcast that told the enemy exactly who was coming. This is how 2 Para won the first great land battle of the Falklands War: a night assault, heavy weapons against fortified trenches, and one of the boldest bluffs in modern military history.
