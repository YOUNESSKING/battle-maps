2 Para's Top 3 Legendary Tactical Moves | The Bluff That Won the Falklands' First Land Battle

Five hundred men. A thousand dug in against them. And the enemy knew they were coming: the day before the attack, the BBC World Service told the entire world that a parachute battalion was poised to attack Darwin and Goose Green.

In this video we break down 2 Para's three greatest tactical moves at Goose Green, 28-29 May 1982: the night assault from Camilla Creek House, the fight for Darwin Hill and Boca House, and the bluff that brought the surrender of the Goose Green garrison.
