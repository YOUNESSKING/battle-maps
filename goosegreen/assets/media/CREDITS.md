## Missing slots (no legally-clear image found — reported per the strict-licence rule)

- **jones_full.png / jones_head.png (Lt Col H Jones VC)**: **No usable image exists.** The only photo used on English Wikipedia (`'H' Jones.jpg`, the infobox portrait) is explicitly tagged **non-free/fair-use** ("This photograph is copyrighted and is NOT under a free license... qualifies as fair use under United States copyright law"), and it is not present on Wikimedia Commons at all — confirmed by a direct Commons search for "Herbert Jones VC", which returned no portrait. No CC/PD/OGL alternative could be found anywhere on Commons. **Do not substitute** — this slot must stay empty or be filled with a non-photographic treatment (e.g. a silhouette, an illustration commissioned/drawn from scratch, or on-screen text) instead of a real photo.
- **keeble_full.png / keeble_head.png (Major Chris Keeble)**: **No image exists at all, free or otherwise.** Neither Wikimedia Commons nor the Chris Keeble Wikipedia article (which has no infobox photo) hold any photograph of him. Same recommendation as above: use a non-photographic treatment or omit the portrait stake for this figure.
- **A dedicated "British troops/paratroopers on the ground in the Falklands, 1982" still**: repeated searches (2 Para, 3 Para, "yomping", Royal Marines, MOD FKD-numbered Imperial War Museum photographs) turned up combat-era ground-troop photography almost exclusively under the **IWM Non-Commercial Licence** (explicitly excluded by the brief) — including one file that was mistakenly tagged `PD-UKGov` on Commons despite being a 1982 photo (Crown Copyright on a 1982 photo would not expire until 2032, and the same file's own source notes "IWM Non-Commercial Licence"); that mistagged file was **not used**. The closest compliant substitutes included instead are the Task Force ships photo (`archive_task_force_ships.jpg`, genuinely OGL) and the Ajax Bay grave photo (`archive_ajax_bay_grave.jpg`, CC BY-SA 3.0, showing 2 Para's dead being buried) — neither is a literal "troops on patrol" shot, so if a true action/ground shot is essential, expect to need a MOD Defence Imagery request or an illustrated/reconstructed visual instead.
