## Description
In the summer of 1780, the American Revolution was dying in the South. Charleston had surrendered with an entire army, and at Camden a second American army was destroyed in a single morning.

In this video we break down Nathanael Greene's three greatest tactical moves: the Race to the Dan, where he led Lord Cornwallis two hundred miles across North Carolina and left him stranded at a river with no boats; the Battle of Guilford Courthouse, where he gave up the field and cost Cornwallis more than a quarter of his army; and the reconquest of the South that ended at Eutaw Springs and sent Cornwallis marching toward Yorktown.

He lost almost every battle he fought, and he took back the entire South.
